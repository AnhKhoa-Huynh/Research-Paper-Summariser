**arXiv** · 2026-11-25 · Score 8.45 · Dexterous manipulation and embodied AI, NLP and language models

The authors introduce RePace, an offline reasoning framework designed to accelerate human demonstrations used for fine-tuning vision-language-action policies. The method uses a pretrained language model to evaluate candidate retiming effects and select a speedup profile for each episode.

**Method:** RePace measures how different candidate speedup factors shorten the demonstration sequence and compares the resulting observation-action pairs. A pretrained language model then interprets these retiming effects using visual context and original motion to select a complete episode speedup profile based on the task instruction.
**Claimed finding:** Experimental results on LIBERO and RoboCasa-Kitchen show that RePace outperforms baselines in overall task execution efficiency, while matching or exceeding acceleration baselines in success rate and completing successful trials faster across three tasks using a Franka Research 3 arm.

The approach combines a physically consistent simulator, a diverse benchmark suite, and a transformer-based policy learning architecture.

**Method:** ViTacSim utilizes a tri-modal tactile primitive anchored to PhysX contact outputs, featuring a global sparse contact-force lattice, a local dense force array, and tactile images, along with local stiffness alignment and decoupled slip-stick regimes.
