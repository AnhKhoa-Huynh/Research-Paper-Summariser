### 7. [Towards Pose-Robust Few-Shot Vision-Language-Action Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24360v1)
**arXiv** · 2026-11-18 · Score 7.85 · Dexterous manipulation and embodied AI, Robotics and autonomy

The authors state that few-shot vision-language-action policies fail when the camera-to-base extrinsic changes due to a bumped tripod or moved robot base. To address this, they introduce WACO, a framework that uses the robot arm and static scene to estimate shifts and canonicalize the inputs for robust policy execution.

**Method:** WACO estimates both camera and robot base shifts from a single RGB-D frame by using the robot arm as a metric calibration target and the static scene as a world anchor, without requiring ground-truth extrinsics or extra data, and fine-tunes the unmodified policy.
**Claimed finding:** WACO consistently outperforms the standard few-shot recipe and prior viewpoint-robust methods across simulated and real-world tasks, raising shifted success rates and recovering most of the performance gap compared to a ground-truth oracle.

**Relevance:** It establishes a new state of the art among methods that use no perturbed training data and require no test-time calibration, significantly improving robot manipulation success under spatial shifts.
