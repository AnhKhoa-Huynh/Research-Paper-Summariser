### 1. [Reins: Frontier Multimodal Models Driving Real Robot Arms on Long-Horizon Tasks, Zero-Shot](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16632v1)
**arXiv** · 2026-11-12 · Score 11.05 · Human–robot interaction, Dexterous manipulation and embodied AI, Robotics and autonomy

The authors introduce Reins, a harness designed to enable off-the-shelf frontier multimodal models to operate dual-arm robots on long-horizon manipulation tasks. Utilizing 3D perception tools, atomic actions, and a continuous narrator instance, the system allows models to execute tasks successfully with natural-language prompts and no task-specific code.

**Method:** An off-the-shelf model operates a dual-arm robot using 3D perception tools and atomic actions that report contact and grip, chaining actions with conditional constraints, while a second model instance acts as a narrator to continuously monitor camera feeds and report changes.
**Claimed finding:** With the Reins harness and natural-language prompts, GPT-6.1 Sol achieves an 88% success rate on real episodes across five tasks, whereas without the harness it fails every shell game and takes eight times as long.

### 2. [NSDL: Neuro-Symbolic Division of Labor for Multi-Robot Task Planning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16671v1)
**arXiv** · 2026-11-12 · Score 10.9 · Swarm and multi-agent robotics, Robotics and autonomy, NLP and language models

The authors propose NSDL, a neuro-symbolic approach for multi-robot task planning that divides responsibilities between a vision-language model and a deterministic solver. The model generates a plan sketch from visual and textual inputs, which the solver then expands into a complete executable joint plan.

**Method:** A small vision-language model writes judgments as a plan sketch and utilizes a detector and a solver as tools, with the model trained with the solver in the loop.
**Claimed finding:** NSDL outperforms end-to-end planners and formalize-then-plan baselines on the VIKI-L2 and LOCUS-Bench benchmarks, with ordering and scheduling errors nearly eliminated.
**Evidence gaps:** Remaining failures are attributed to misjudged states, and missed goals are mostly those the model never lists.
**Relevance:** It demonstrates that combining vision-language models for scene-based judgments with deterministic solvers for computational planning effectively improves multi-robot task planning performance.
**Summary basis:** gemini from abstract

By breaking the global optimization problem into smaller subproblems through iterative clustering, the approach achieves monotonic utility improvement and outperforms several baseline methods in simulation.

**Method:** Iterative clustering algorithm that partitions the global optimization problem into smaller subproblems by iteratively forming clusters of robots and tasks, and optimizing assignments within each cluster while keeping robots clustered with their currently assigned tasks.
