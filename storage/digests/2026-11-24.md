### 5. [DexLatentWorld: 3D Cross-Embodiment Latent Action World Models for Zero-Shot Robotic Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30907v1)
**arXiv** · 2026-11-23 · Score 8.8 · Dexterous manipulation and embodied AI, Robotics and autonomy, AI and machine learning

The authors introduce a 3D latent-action world model designed to learn continuous action representations from human manipulation demonstrations using point-cloud observations. This model aims to provide a unified framework that enables zero-shot robotic manipulation across different embodiments without requiring embodiment-specific action decoders.

**Method:** The authors employ a contrastive objective to learn consistent motion representations across embodiments from point-cloud observations of retargeted human demonstrations, using the resulting unified latent action space for model-predictive control and recovering executable robot commands from predicted geometric motion.
**Claimed finding:** The authors claim the model achieves 90%, 92%, and 60% success on reaching, grasping, and placing respectively, and an 86% success rate across 50 trials for zero-shot real-world manipulation with an unseen dexterous hand.
