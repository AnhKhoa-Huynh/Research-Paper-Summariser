### 3. [World In-Context Learning for Agentic Robot Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12885v1)
**arXiv** · 2026-11-09 · Score 10.9 · Dexterous manipulation and embodied AI, Robotics and autonomy, AI and machine learning

The authors introduce World In-Context Learning (WICL) and its implementation, RoboScript, to address the distribution coupling limitations of traditional action policies. By allowing a frozen model to rehearse tasks in a simulation world and store feedback in context rather than weights, the approach achieves strong task transfer and high success rates across evaluated benchmarks.

**Method:** RoboScript implements WICL by taking a single demonstration, rehearsing the task through simulation, freezing the acquired knowledge into a Task Memory, and deploying it on new scenes with data isolation matching real robot constraints.
**Claimed finding:** RoboScript achieves an 83.4% mean success rate across 50 tasks in RoboTwin 2.0 with one demonstration per task and no gradient updates, outperforming the strongest trained action policy by over 20 points.
**Evidence gaps:** Not specified in source
**Relevance:** The proposed paradigm demonstrates that with a world in the loop, a robot can learn manipulation tasks effectively in its context, generalizing across visual distributions and transferring to physical dual-arm robots.
