The authors introduce MG-CD-MPM, a simulator designed to handle complex multi-state material dynamics and two-way tool-material contact for reinforcement learning. The system enables efficient modeling of soft solids, brittle materials, granular media, and viscoplastic substances within a unified framework.

**Method:** The method uses a tool-centered localized extension of CD-MPM where an implicit Ginzburg-Landau phase-field damage system is solved on a local patch via preconditioned conjugate gradients using a geometric-multigrid V-cycle preconditioner, combined with a uniform background MPM discretization and two-way rigid-tool coupling.
**Claimed finding:** The simulator successfully supports six demonstrations spanning soft-to-brittle cutting scenes, viscoplastic scooping, and granular shoveling, and the authors RL-train PPO agents on tofu and peanut brittle cutting tasks.

**OpenAlex** · 2026-11-13 · Score 2.0 · AI and machine learning

This paper investigates exit timing strategies for SPX short-straddle portfolios using a combined supervised and reinforcement learning approach. The authors demonstrate that initializing reinforcement learning actors with a supervised multilayer perceptron improves out-of-sample trading performance compared to training from scratch or using traditional exit rules.

**Method:** A two-stage framework utilizing historical episodes to generate ex-post optimal hold or exit labels, which train a multilayer perceptron using contemporaneously available state variables, followed by reinforcement-learning actors fine-tuned with a risk-aware trading reward.
**Claimed finding:** Out-of-sample evaluation over 2024-2026 shows that the MLP-initialized Actor-Critic achieves an average cumulative return of 37.54% and a Sharpe ratio of 1.705, outperforming the supervised MLP alone and Actor-Critic trained from scratch.

**Relevance:** The findings demonstrate that oracle-guided supervised learning offers a stable foundation for reward-based policy fine-tuning in sparse and path-dependent option-exit problems.
