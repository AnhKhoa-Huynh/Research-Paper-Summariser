### 3. [RoboTryOn: Synthesizing Dexterous Robot Demonstrations from Human Videos via Virtual Try-On](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05163v1)
**arXiv** · 2026-11-03 · Score 10.7 · Dexterous manipulation and embodied AI, Robotics and autonomy

The authors propose RoboTryOn, a framework that translates egocentric human videos into videos of a target robot to bypass the need for teleoperated demonstrations in robotic control. By treating the translation as a virtual try-on problem, the system preserves the original task, objects, and background while swapping the human for a specified robot embodiment.

**Method:** The approach translates selected keyframes autoregressively to guide video synthesis conditioned on the human hand skeleton, and employs an inverse dynamics model utilizing depth features to extract action labels from synthesized videos for policy learning.
**Claimed finding:** Policies trained on synthetic data generated by RoboTryOn increase the average success rate on novel tasks from 2% to 60% across multiple humanoid embodiments without any real robot demonstrations, and achieve performance comparable to policies trained on teleoperated demonstrations at about one-third of the collection cost.
**Evidence gaps:** Not specified in source
**Relevance:** It provides a cost-efficient scalable substitute for teleoperated demonstrations by enabling robot foundation models to learn new manipulation tasks directly from human videos.
