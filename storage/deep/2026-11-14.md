## [DexSeed: Expanding Human Hand-Object Interactions into Robot Demonstrations for Dexterous Policy Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14092v1)

**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14092v1
**Status:** reviewed
**Pages:** 9/9
**Coverage:** full extractable PDF text

**Research question:** How to generate diverse, physically executable robot demonstrations at scale from human hand-object interactions to support dexterous vision-language-action (VLA) policy learning?

**Claimed contribution:** The authors present DexSeed, a framework that organizes human interactions by grasp mode and expands individual sequences into diverse robot demonstrations paired with language descriptions, an InteractionMesh-based retargeting and cross-object transfer method, physics-based imitation policies for executable rollouts, and a robot-centric dataset of 25270 demonstrations from 19 human sequences spanning 8 grasp modes.

**Method:** The framework selects human interaction sequences from the GRAB dataset, constructs a grasp bank with GPT-generated descriptions, applies InteractionMesh retargeting to adapt human interactions to robot hands and target object geometries, augments whole-arm trajectories, trains RL finger-control policies in IsaacLab via physics-based imitation under arm-tracking dynamics, and renders realistic visual observations using 3D Gaussian Splatting.

**Datasets/evaluation:** The GRAB dataset is used as the human source data (19 sequences spanning 8 representative grasp modes and 17 object categories), resulting in a generated robot-centric dataset of 25270 demonstrations.

**Baselines:** Dex-Retargeting, SPIDER, Wuji official retargeter, ManipTrans, and Dexplore.

**Reported findings:** The proposed retargeting method outperforms baselines with a contact error of 3.326 mm, angular error of 7.636 degrees, and penetration depth of 3.371 mm. In simulation VLA pick-and-place success rates reach 97.50 percent on seen factors and 91.25 percent on fully unseen factors (layouts, geometries, and appearances), outperforming ManipTrans (51.25 percent) and Dexplore (32.50 percent) data. Real-world deployment on a Franka arm and Wuji hand demonstrates successful execution across all eight grasp modes.

**Limitations/threats to validity:** The work focuses primarily on representative grasp modes and pick-and-place tasks, leaving in-hand manipulation, bimanual tasks, different robot hand embodiments, and tactile observations for future work.

**Reproducibility checklist:** The project page is available at https://robbyant-research.github.io/DexSeed/, but explicit open-source code repositories or model weights are not detailed in the text beyond mentioning IsaacLab, LingBot-VLA, and simulation environments.

**Recommended follow-up experiments:** Future work could extend the framework to in-hand manipulation with changing contacts, bimanual tasks such as object handovers, robot hands with alternative finger structures, and incorporation of tactile observations to detect slip.
