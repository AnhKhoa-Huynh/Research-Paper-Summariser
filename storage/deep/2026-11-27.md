## [Simulator-as-Proxy World Model for Robot Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32786v1)

**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32786v1
**Status:** reviewed
**Pages:** 27/27
**Coverage:** full extractable PDF text

**Research question:** Can video world models strictly follow robot actions, and how can their reliance on scene states and physical plausibility be tested?

**Claimed contribution:** The paper introduces SimPWM (Simulator-as-Proxy World Model), which uses a rule-based point simulator to generate an explicit state and proxy from the first frame, allowing video models to be tested against physical consequences.

**Method:** SimPWM splits scene perception from video generation by using a calibrated camera and SAM 2/3 to lift the first frame into a point set, applying a rule-based point simulator driven by actions without trained parameters, and fine-tuning video models to translate the proxy into a realistic video.

**Datasets/evaluation:** DROID (293 test episodes from unseen setups, 3,000 training episodes), WorldArena, and GR1 teleoperation data.

**Baselines:** Ctrl-World, DreamDojo, Cosmos-Predict 2.5, PointWorld, and Agentic Real2Sim.

**Reported findings:** SimPWM variants achieve higher robot IoU (0.77-0.78 vs. at most 0.69 for end-to-end models) and lower FVD (85.6) on DROID test episodes, while simulated objects reduce missed pick-ups by 16.5 points compared to robot-only proxies.

**Limitations/threats to validity:** The simulator does not model pushing, pouring, or articulation, ropes and cloth are carried rigidly, poor calibration or monocular depth lowers accuracy, and actions are evaluated open-loop rather than closed-loop.

**Reproducibility checklist:** Code and project pages are publicly provided on GitHub and via a web link, though specific details rely on pretrained foundational modules like SAM 2/3 and foundation stereo matching.

**Recommended follow-up experiments:** Future work could extend the framework to model complex contact interactions such as pushing, pouring, and fabric articulation, or integrate closed-loop policy execution.
