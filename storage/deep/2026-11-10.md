## [World In-Context Learning for Agentic Robot Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12885v1)

**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12885v1
**Status:** reviewed
**Pages:** 23/33
**Coverage:** partial/truncated text

**Research question:** Can a robot learn a manipulation task in its context as well as it learns one in its weights?

**Claimed contribution:** Formulates World In-Context Learning (WICL) to decouple task skill from network weights, builds RoboScript as a modular infrastructure with strict data isolation, and demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on RoboTwin 2.0 without gradient updates.

**Method:** RoboScript implements WICL by starting from a single demonstration, performing task learning through imitation and rehearsal in a simulator, storing semantic knowledge in a frozen Task Memory, and deploying with anticipation, recovery, and recursive self-improvement.

**Datasets/evaluation:** RoboTwin 2.0 benchmark consisting of 50 tasks evaluated in both clean and randomized settings.

**Baselines:** StarVLA, GR00T-N1.7, GalaxeaVLA, Xiaomi-Robotics-0, Abot-M0, X-VLA, Spatial Forcing, Γ0.5, AHA-WAM, FastWAM, X-WAM, LingBot-VA, 4D-WAM, and HarnessVLA.

**Reported findings:** RoboScript reaches 83.4% mean success across clean and randomized settings on RoboTwin 2.0 with one demonstration per task and no gradient updates, surpassing the strongest trained action policy (61.7%).

**Limitations/threats to validity:** Relies on commercial foundation models via APIs with varying service quality, and exhibits slow inference speed requiring multiple model calls per episode.

**Reproducibility checklist:** Code is available at https://github.com/ArcheOne-AI/RoboScript and the project page is at https://ArcheOne-AI.github.io/RoboScript.

**Recommended follow-up experiments:** Exploring faster local models for inference, replacing simulators with learned world models, and scaling cross-embodiment adaptation to a wider range of physical robots.
