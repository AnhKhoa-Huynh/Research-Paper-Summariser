## [ReContact: Reconstruct and Retarget Human Contacts from Video for Contact-Rich Humanoid Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02968v1)

**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.02968v1
**Status:** reviewed
**Pages:** 14/14
**Coverage:** full extractable PDF text

**Research question:** How can humanoid robots robustly reconstruct and retarget contact-rich manipulation tasks from human videos, and execute complex support transitions without falling into local optima like accidental side-squeezes?

**Claimed contribution:** The paper proposes ReContact, a system that reconstructs and retargets human-object interactions through an object-centric contact plan, utilizing contact priors, surface-constrained retargeting, a residual policy rewarded for contact anchors and directions, and a task-family reference generation method.

**Method:** ReContact uses an object-centric contact plan detailing contact pairs, anchors, pressing directions, and stick/slide modes. It employs contact priors for object pose tracking through occlusion, surface-constrained whole-body retargeting, and a residual reinforcement learning controller rewarded for making contact near planned anchors along specified directions.

**Datasets/evaluation:** The evaluation utilizes simulation tasks including an under-arm box carry, a thigh-supported regrasp, under-arm holds of a tissue pack and a shuttlecock tube, tube-transfer family generation across 247 references, and irregular-object grasps from egocentric video (spray bottle and banana).

**Baselines:** Baselines include HDMI-style, ResMimic-style, and CWS adapter (incorporating CHORD's wrench-space objective) evaluated under the same reference trajectories.

**Reported findings:** In simulation on the thigh-supported regrasp, ReContact achieves the planned two-palm bottom hold in 83.5 ± 5.2% of episodes over three training seeds, compared to 0.16% for the best adapted baseline. Training one policy on a 247-reference family raises in-workspace tube-transfer success from 20.4% to 97.8%, and the method is successfully demonstrated on a physical Unitree G1 across nine tasks.

**Limitations/threats to validity:** Not reported

**Reproducibility checklist:** The paper provides detailed mathematical formulations for the contact plan, objective functions, reward definitions, and algorithm steps, along with a project page link (https://recontact-robot.github.io).

**Recommended follow-up experiments:** Future work includes investigating concurrent skills on different body surfaces, grasping while walking and foot-terrain contacts, and developing a plan-conditioned controller that executes contact transitions from new videos without retraining a policy.

## [KlaskTron-IL: A Contact-Rich Adversarial Imitation Learning Benchmark with a Validated Sim-to-Real Bridge](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03091v1)

**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03091v1
**Status:** reviewed
**Pages:** 13/13
**Coverage:** full extractable PDF text

**Research question:** What happens when standard imitation learning is run on the KlaskTron platform under contact-rich adversarial dynamics?

**Claimed contribution:** KlaskTron-IL: a multi-tier imitation learning dataset and diagnostic baselines exposing three characteristic failure modes under contact-rich adversarial dynamics with a validated sim-to-real bridge.

**Method:** Diagnostic evaluation of behavioral cloning (BC), generative adversarial imitation learning (GAIL), and BC-bootstrapped RL (BC+RL) using a multi-tier dataset spanning human hardware demonstrations, symmetry augmentation, and synthetic expert trajectories.

**Datasets/evaluation:** Tier 1: 80,591 human demonstration timesteps recorded on the physical robot; Tier 2: 322,364 symmetry-augmented timesteps; Tier 3: 5,000,000 synthetic expert trajectories from a PPO policy.

**Baselines:** Behavioral Cloning (BC), Generative Adversarial Imitation Learning (GAIL), and BC-bootstrapped PPO (BC+RL).

**Reported findings:** BC exhibits a data-scaling plateau across 80k to 5M demonstrations due to covariate shift; GAIL learns a discriminator that recovers proximity with Spearman rho = -1.0 but features a logit span under 0.1; BC-bootstrapped RL shows an asymmetric prior x reward-density interaction where the BC prior stabilizes sparse reward training but has no detectable effect under dense shaping.

**Limitations/threats to validity:** The observation space is restricted to a 12D state vector, magnetic biscuit obstacles are omitted, human action labels are derived from finite-differenced position estimates, the PPO expert does not represent ceiling performance, and zero-shot hardware transfer is demonstrated only for the PPO expert and a single BC policy.

**Reproducibility checklist:** Code and dataset are publicly available via HuggingFace and GitHub, including Isaac Lab environments, training pipelines, evaluation harnesses, and inherited actuator models.

**Recommended follow-up experiments:** Future work includes reequipping the discriminator with observation history, investigating methods that address covariate shift like DAgger or diffusion policies, and deploying improved IL baselines on physical hardware.
