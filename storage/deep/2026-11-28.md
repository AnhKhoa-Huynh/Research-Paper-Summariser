## [DiffGR00T: Execution-Aware Differentiable Training for Humanoid Whole-Body Vision-Language-Action Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32665v1)

**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32665v1
**Status:** reviewed
**Pages:** 14/14
**Coverage:** full extractable PDF text

**Research question:** How to enable execution-aware differentiable training for humanoid whole-body vision-language-action models by coupling a flow-matching VLA with a whole-body foundation model through a learned world model?

**Claimed contribution:** The paper proposes DiffGR00T, an end-to-end differentiable training framework that couples GR00T N1.7 with SONIC using an action-conditioned differentiable world model, raising average simulation task success from 59.2% to 71.7%.

**Method:** DiffGR00T replaces the simulator during training with an action-conditioned differentiable world model, allowing multi-step body-execution losses to backpropagate through a frozen whole-body controller and a flow-matching sampler directly into the VLA action head.

**Datasets/evaluation:** Four HumanoidArena simulation tasks (OpenDoor, SitSofa, Boxing, and VisionNavi) using 400 recorded HumanoidArena episodes carrying SONIC motion tokens (100 per task), plus a world-model data mix comprising clean controller-excitation replay, token-perturbed replay, closed-loop pairs, and branch groups.

**Baselines:** The supervised base VLA (task fine-tuned GR00T N1.7 checkpoint with a frozen SONIC decoder and no adapters).

**Reported findings:** On four HumanoidArena simulation tasks, DiffGR00T improves average task success from 59.2% to 71.7% (+12.5 percentage points) and reduces the step cost per successful episode from 3102 to 2490.

**Limitations/threats to validity:** Quantitative evidence is confined to four simulated tasks, real-robot runs are qualitative only, the learned world model is a coarse simulator rather than an exact physics engine, the world model is not updated on-policy, and object-centric contact as well as 14 hand channels are not directly optimized by the execution objective.

**Reproducibility checklist:** The architecture, hyperparameters, and training recipes are detailed in text and tables (such as Table III), using two RTX 4090 GPUs, 300 updates, batch size 32, and LoRA rank 16.

**Recommended follow-up experiments:** Improving the world model with velocity-aware prediction heads, kinematic consistency constraints, and contact-aware state representations, updating the world model on-policy, and extending the differentiable treatment to the 14 hand joints.
