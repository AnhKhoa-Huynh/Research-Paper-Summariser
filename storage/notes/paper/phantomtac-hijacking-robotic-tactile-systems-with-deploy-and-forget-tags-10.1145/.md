## Summary / source snapshot

The authors identify a physical vulnerability termed PhantomTac in magnetic tactile sensors that allows attackers to manipulate force interpretation. By utilizing thin, battery-free magnetic tags concealed on object surfaces, the attack can disrupt robotic gripping and cause object damage or dropping.

## Method / approach

The researchers performed reverse-engineering to characterize commercial-off-the-shelf tactile sensors, proposed a practical tag design, and conducted end-to-end evaluations on real robots and robotic arms.

## Reported contribution / result

The proposed attack achieves a success rate exceeding 95% when the grasping-position offset is within 8 mm, and successfully bypasses existing anti-interference mechanisms.
