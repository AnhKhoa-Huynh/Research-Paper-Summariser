## Summary / source snapshot

Video world models are used as simulators, so when a first frame leaves several physical outcomes open, repeated generations should produce each outcome as often as it occurs in the data. Physics post-training scores every generation against the single recorded future of its history, which makes single videos more faithful, but the expected score is linear in the model distribution and is maximized by always producing the most likely outcome.
