## Summary / source snapshot

Cellulose-based gels are emerging as promising soft materials for tactile electronic skin (e-skin) because of their tunable network structures, mechanical compliance, sustainability, and compatibility with ionic and electronic conduction. This review examines recent progress from material and structural engineering to tactile sensing, machine learning (ML)-assisted signal interpretation, and robotic manipulation.
