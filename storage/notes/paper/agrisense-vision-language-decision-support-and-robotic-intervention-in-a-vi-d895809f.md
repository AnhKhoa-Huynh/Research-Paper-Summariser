## Summary / source snapshot

Autonomous greenhouse management increasingly requires robotic systems that integrate perception, navigation, environmental regulation, and plant-level intervention. This paper presents AgriSense, a framework combining an Isaac Sim virtual greenhouse, a quadruped mobile manipulator, RGB and LiDAR-inertial sensing, environmental control, and supervised vision-language decision support.
