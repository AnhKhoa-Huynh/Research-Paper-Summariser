## Summary / source snapshot

Industry 4.0, or the Fourth Industrial Revolution, denotes the integration of cyber-physical systems, the Internet of Things, artificial intelligence, big-data analytics, additive manufacturing, and advanced robotics into manufacturing and industrial processes. For small, hydrocarbon-reliant economies such as the Kingdom of Bahrain, the adoption of these technologies is closely linked to economic diversification, fiscal sustainability, and private-sector employment objectives
