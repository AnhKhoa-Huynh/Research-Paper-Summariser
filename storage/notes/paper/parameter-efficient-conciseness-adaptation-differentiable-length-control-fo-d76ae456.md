Smaller language models (SLMs) make it feasible to deploy clinical natural language processing where large proprietary models are too costly, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning allows them to be specialised on commodity hardware. One obstacle persists after training: on medical tasks these models answer at far greater length than the clinical question requires.
