## Summary / source snapshot

Autonomous racing requires an accurate vehicle dynamics model near the limits of handling, yet a mismatch between the nominal and actual dynamics inevitably remains. To reduce this mismatch, existing methods learn from on-track data, i.e., data collected while the vehicle drives on the track under racing conditions, by adapting the physical parameters of the nominal model or by learning a residual that captures the remaining mismatch.
