## Summary / source snapshot

A robot's actions change the thermal conditions of its next interaction: heat retained after release, carried with objects or stored beneath the surface can change a later contact's outcome despite similar current temperatures. Training trajectories must capture how these effects connect earlier actions to later observations and task outcomes.
