## Summary / source snapshot

General-purpose robotic manipulation requires task reasoning, failure recovery, and precise control, yet limited manipulation data make these abilities difficult to obtain from a single policy. Recent multimodal agents show promise in task reasoning and recovery but lack reliable fine-grained control and incur high inference cost, whereas learned policies provide efficient fine-grained execution but struggle beyond their learned skills.
