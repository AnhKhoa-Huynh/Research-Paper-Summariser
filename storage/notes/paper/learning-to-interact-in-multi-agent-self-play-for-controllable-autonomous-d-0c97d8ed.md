## Summary / source snapshot

Large-scale, multi-agent self-play trains robust driving policies effectively in simulation, but controlling these models to generate specific, safety-critical edge cases remains a key challenge. Existing goal-conditioned self-play frameworks rely primarily on static map-location goals and fail to reliably produce complex agent interactions such as aggressive tailgating or adversarial cut-offs.
