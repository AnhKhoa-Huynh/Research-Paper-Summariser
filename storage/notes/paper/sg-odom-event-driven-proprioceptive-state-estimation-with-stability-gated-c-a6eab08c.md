## Summary / source snapshot

Reliable proprioceptive state estimation for legged robots fuses inertial sensing with kinematic sensing from leg encoders. The latter assumes a foot is stationary during the stance phase; however, physical foot touchdown does not necessarily provide a stationary reference for odometry, particularly during impact and load transfer.
