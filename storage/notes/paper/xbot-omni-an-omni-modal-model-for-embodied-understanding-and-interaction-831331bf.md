## Summary / source snapshot

Embodied interaction requires an agent to integrate multimodal perception with verbal and nonverbal behaviors to effectively communicate its intentions, including speech, body motion, and facial expressions. We present XBot-Omni, a Thinker--Talker--Actor architecture that extends the Omni paradigm to robotic modalities, including body motion and facial expressions, with ``X'' representing the extensibility to robotic-specific modalities.
