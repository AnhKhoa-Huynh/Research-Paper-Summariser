## Summary / source snapshot

The authors propose LoRe-MIM, a self-supervised masked image modeling framework designed to learn multimodal representations from a curated heterogeneous cohort of whole-body PET/CT scans. By employing a masking strategy that either zeros out or cross-modally substitutes image regions, the method enables the capture of both modality-specific features and cross-modal relationships.

## Method / approach

A masking strategy where image regions are either replaced with zeros or substituted with the corresponding complementary imaging modality, evaluated across tasks such as image reconstruction, cancer classification, lesion segmentation, label-efficient training, and robustness assessment.

## Reported contribution / result

The proposed LoRe-MIM framework consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines across multiple downstream evaluation tasks.
