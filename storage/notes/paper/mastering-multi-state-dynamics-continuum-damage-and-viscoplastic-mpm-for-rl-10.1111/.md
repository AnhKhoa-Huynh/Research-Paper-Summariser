## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce MG-CD-MPM, a simulator designed to handle complex multi-state material dynamics and two-way tool-material contact for reinforcement learning. The system enables efficient modeling of soft solids, brittle materials, granular media, and viscoplastic substances within a unified framework.

## Method / approach

The method uses a tool-centered localized extension of CD-MPM where an implicit Ginzburg-Landau phase-field damage system is solved on a local patch via preconditioned conjugate gradients using a geometric-multigrid V-cycle preconditioner, combined with a uniform background MPM discretization and two-way rigid-tool coupling.

## Reported contribution / result

The simulator successfully supports six demonstrations spanning soft-to-brittle cutting scenes, viscoplastic scooping, and granular shoveling, and the authors RL-train PPO agents on tofu and peanut brittle cutting tasks.
