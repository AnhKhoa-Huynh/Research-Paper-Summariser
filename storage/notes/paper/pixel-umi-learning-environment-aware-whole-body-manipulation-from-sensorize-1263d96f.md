## Summary / source snapshot

The paper introduces Pixel-UMI, a pipeline designed to learn environment-aware whole-body manipulation policies using sensorized human demonstrations. It addresses challenges related to embodiment differences, viewpoint mismatches, and contact-rich interactions through specialized retargeting, masking, and data correction techniques.

## Method / approach

The approach utilizes Environment-Aware Retargeting to generate collision-aware joint and base targets from scene geometry and trajectories, delay-calibrated action assignment, Retargeting-Error-Aware Masking to handle viewpoint mismatches, and on-policy human corrections collected via DAgger.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim that Pixel-UMI achieves an average task progress of 87.0 percent across five challenging tasks including articulated-object manipulation, deformable-object handling, constrained-space reaching, whole-body collision avoidance, and contact-rich bimanual coordination.
