## Summary / source snapshot

In vision-based teleoperation of robotic hands, the operator receives no haptic feedback and therefore struggles to regulate the grasp force, while adding tactile sensors to the fingers increases wiring, cost and integration complexity. Compliant hands offer an alternative, as their mechanical compliance, usually treated only as a passive property for safe and adaptive grasping, can also serve as a source of information.
