## Summary / source snapshot

Robot policies predominantly learn from human-collected data emphasizing competent behavior, limiting coverage of the low-level behaviors needed to support autonomous improvement. We study how a form of midtraining can be used to build coverage over diverse low-level behaviors that connect challenging states of the robot and its environment to states from which further progress is possible ("connected behavioral coverage").
