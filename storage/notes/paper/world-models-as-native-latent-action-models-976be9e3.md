## Summary / source snapshot

Large and diverse video sources, such as egocentric human video and heterogeneous robot recordings, carry rich dynamics but come either without actions or without a shared action space. Latent action models (LAMs) can recover these dynamics, but they train their inverse- and forward-dynamics models (IDM, FDM) from scratch, trading off pixel reconstruction against latent capacity and accuracy.
