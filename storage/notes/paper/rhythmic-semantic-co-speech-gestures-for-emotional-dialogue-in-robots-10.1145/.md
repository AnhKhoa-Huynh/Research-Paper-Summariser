## Summary / source snapshot

Most data-driven robot systems generate either rhythmic beat-like motion or semantically meaningful iconic gestures, but not both. We present an emotion-aware rhythmic–semantic co-speech gesture system that generates iconic and beat behaviors in real time from text and the robot’s internal emotional state.
