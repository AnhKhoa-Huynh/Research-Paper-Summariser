## Summary / source snapshot

Collective estimation under unreliable sensing requires robots to adaptively balance personal observations and social information from peers. We propose DGF-Asym, a decentralized collective estimation method extending the DeGroot-Friedkin model with asymmetric peer weighting based on retrospective predictive performance.
