## Summary / source snapshot

A general-purpose Vision-Language Model (VLM) has surpassed expert vision models in many domains such as object detection and video recognition. However, point correspondence is known to be challenging for VLMs since it goes beyond object-level recognition: it requires pixel-level localization of the same physical surface point across observations, even when appearance changes or the point disappears and later reappears.
