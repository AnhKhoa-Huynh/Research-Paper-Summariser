## Summary / source snapshot

This paper investigates the vulnerability of vision-language-action models in robotic manipulation to physically realizable adversarial 3D textures. The authors introduce Tex3D, a framework that utilizes Foreground-Background Decoupling and Trajectory-Aware Adversarial Optimization to enable end-to-end differentiable texture optimization.

## Method / approach

Foreground-Background Decoupling for differentiable texture optimization via dual-renderer alignment, and Trajectory-Aware Adversarial Optimization using prioritized behaviorally critical frames and vertex-based parameterization.

## Reported contribution / result

Tex3D significantly degrades vision-language-action model performance across multiple manipulation tasks, achieving task failure rates of up to 96.7% in both simulation and real-robot settings.
