## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce a benchmark called Seeing What Matters to evaluate task-conditioned actionable spatial belief in simulated tabletop manipulation. The study measures whether view-selection policies can acquire specific visual evidence required by a manipulation command rather than relying on general scene description.

## Method / approach

The benchmark provides 860 frozen evaluation cases across three domains using two protocols: closed-loop actionability and same-object functional out-of-distribution generalization. The authors also test a diagnostic reference pipeline and seven vision-language models on these cases.

## Reported contribution / result

The reference pipeline achieves 98.8% closed-loop and 98.9% functional-OOD success at primary budget settings, whereas seven evaluated vision-language models only reach 26 to 43 percent.

## Why it might matter
