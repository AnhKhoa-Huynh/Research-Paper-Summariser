## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce TwinRL, a digital twin-driven reinforcement learning framework designed to improve real-world robotic manipulation for Vision-Language-Action models. The approach combines supervised fine-tuning warm-up, digital twin reinforcement learning warm-up, and real-world reinforcement learning to expand exploration coverage and efficiency.

## Method / approach

A three-stage framework comprising supervised fine-tuning warm-up with an exploration space expansion strategy, parallel reinforcement learning warm-up in a smartphone-reconstructed digital twin acting as an exploration guide, and real-world reinforcement learning with targeted human-in-the-loop rollouts.

## Reported contribution / result

TwinRL achieves near-100% success rates in both in-distribution and out-of-distribution regions across four tasks, while delivering over 30% faster convergence than prior real-world reinforcement learning methods using only 20 minutes of on-robot interaction.
