## Summary / source snapshot

Dexterous hand design demands repeated expert work across geometry, robot-description code, collision debugging, grasp initialization, and policy training. We present Task2Hand, a Skill: a specification package that turns a functional manipulation brief into a parameterized hand model and routes every candidate through cost-ordered contact, dynamics, and learning gates.
