## Summary / source snapshot

High accuracy on streaming video benchmarks does not establish whether a model uses visual history. Our review of OVO-Bench and StreamingBench finds that recent frames suffice for 32% of sampled historical questions, while models also answer many questions correctly from the question and options alone.
