## Summary / source snapshot

Robust visual servoing during robotic grasping remains challenging, as approach-phase occlusion frequently disrupts closed-loop control. To address this, we present an active observation framework in which one arm of an existing dual-arm platform carries an RGB-D camera as an active observer while the other executes grasping.
