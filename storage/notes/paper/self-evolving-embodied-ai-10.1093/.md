## Summary / source snapshot

This paper introduces self-evolving embodied AI, a new paradigm designed to overcome the limitations of human-crafted settings in traditional embodied AI. The proposed framework employs software-level components and a hardware-level component to achieve continually adaptive intelligence through software-hardware co-evolution.

## Method / approach

The authors present the definition, framework, components, mechanisms, roadmap, and evaluation indicators of self-evolving embodied AI, alongside a systematic review of state-of-the-art works for realized components, discussions of practical applications, and future research directions.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim that self-evolving embodied AI enables agents to autonomously learn and interact with environments in a human-like manner.
