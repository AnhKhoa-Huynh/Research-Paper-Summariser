## Summary / source snapshot

Public outdoor mixed reality (MR) exhibitions make guidance a spatial interaction problem: visitors need timely explanations while roaming open sites, attending to virtual artworks, hazards, bystanders, and route choices. We present Dream-Butterfly, a field-deployed embodied conversational guide for campus-scale outdoor MR exhibitions.
