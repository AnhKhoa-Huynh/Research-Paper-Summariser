## Summary / source snapshot

This paper proposes a novel class of Bayesian Conjugate Distributions (BCDs) on extended special Euclidean groups SE_n(3) for global motion uncertainty representation and estimation. BCDs are constructed by restricting matrix Gaussian distributions on ambient Euclidean space to SE_n(3), avoiding singularity of local coordinates.
