## Summary / source snapshot

Driving vision-language-action (VLA) models can accept navigation instructions, yet predicting a driver's trajectory does not establish that a model follows the instruction. We study how driving-language supervision is collected and evaluated, distinguishing prospective instructions issued before an action from retrospective labels written after observing it.
