## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce an origami-based tactile pad designed to simultaneously measure pressure and perceive materials using internal and external triboelectric nanogenerators. When integrated onto a soft gripper, the sensor fuses pressure and material signals to accurately identify a set of geometrically similar objects.

## Method / approach

The authors employ an origami-based structure featuring internal Z-TENGs on a zigzag skeleton for pressure measurement and an external M-TENG to capture surface material features. They also integrate this tactile pad onto a soft gripper for grasping state detection and object recognition.

## Reported contribution / result

The tactile pad achieves a pressure sensitivity of 9.74 V kPa-1 with high linearity and enables the identification of 15 geometrically similar objects with 97.33% accuracy by fusing pressure and material signals.
