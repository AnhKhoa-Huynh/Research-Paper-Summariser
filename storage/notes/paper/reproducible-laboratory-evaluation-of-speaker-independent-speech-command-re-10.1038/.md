## Summary / source snapshot

Voice-controlled humanoid robots can take over hazardous tasks in defence and field scenarios where direct human intervention is risky. Most reported prototypes couple an Android front end to an Arduino through Bluetooth, which restricts on-board signal processing, obscures end-to-end timing, and is seldom evaluated under speaker-independent conditions.
