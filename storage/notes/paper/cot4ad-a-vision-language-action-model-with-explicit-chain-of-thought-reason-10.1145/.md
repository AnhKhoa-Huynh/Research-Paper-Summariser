## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce CoT4AD, a vision-language-action framework designed to improve numerical and causal reasoning in autonomous driving systems. The model incorporates a structured reasoning process during training and implicit reasoning during inference to handle complex driving scenarios.

## Method / approach

The framework integrates visual observations and language instructions to perform semantic reasoning, scene understanding, and trajectory planning by explicitly modeling a perception-question-prediction-action chain-of-thought during training and implicit reasoning during inference.

## Reported contribution / result

Experiments across real-world and simulated benchmarks such as nuScenes and Bench2Drive show that the proposed approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in open-loop and closed-loop evaluations.
