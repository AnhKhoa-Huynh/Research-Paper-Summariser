## Summary / source snapshot

This study examines the challenges of transferring decentralized swarm robotics code from simulation to physical platforms during live artistic performances. The authors evaluate the execution across tabletop, aerial, and mobile manipulator swarms to understand hardware discrepancies and human perception.

## Method / approach

Deploying the same decentralized coordination code across three different robotic swarm platforms (tabletop, aerial, and human-scale mobile manipulators) during live artistic performances and evaluating them using physical observations and audience surveys.

## Reported contribution / result

Physical embodiment altered visible motion and execution timescales significantly, and audience surveys showed that human emotional responses and meaning attribution did not mirror technical success or vary together.
