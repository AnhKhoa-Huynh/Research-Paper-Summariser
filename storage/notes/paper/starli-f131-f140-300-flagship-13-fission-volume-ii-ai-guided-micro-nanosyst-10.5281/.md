The Ten Research Directions F131 AI-Driven Microfluidic Sampling Strategy Optimization At The Limit — Examines recoverable analyte as a fraction of input under fixed sampling resources. Expert tuning with equal trials is the comparator; hidden human intervention is the rival. Blind recovery audits and out-of-distribution refusal test whether AI planning adds genuine value. F132 Nano-Quantum System Counterfactual Inference At The Limit — Tests held-out, intervention-specific counterfactual prediction error while retaining constraint-violation safeguards. Frozen open-loop policies anchor comparison; training-test leakage and nonreplayable observation-to-action chains can invalidate apparent autonomy. F133 AI-Driven Microrobotic Autonomous Calibration At The Limit — Uses blind-standard calibration bias after autonomous correction as a physical measurement target. Preregistered experimental design is the baseline. Extra experimental trials and any action outside the authority envelope defeat a claimed improvement. F134 Microscale Physical Model and Neural Network Coupling At The Limit — Challenges coupled learning and physical models against independently measured conservation residuals under withheld shifts. Matched expert tuning and hidden manual intervention are alternative explanations; unsafe confidence outside the tested domain is a stop condition. F135 AI-Driven Nanoscale Sampling Failure Prediction At The Limit — Proposes prospective fault warning lead time, in seconds, at a fixed false-alarm rate. Frozen open-loop policies and unselected failure cases distinguish early warning from hindsight leakage. Recovery outcomes and complete action replay remain necessary. F136 Quantum Sensor Interpretable AI At The Limit — Asks whether AI explanations agree with mechanism-specific physical ablations, rather than merely producing plausible narratives. The proposed witness is an agreement fraction, with counterfactual error and calibrated sensing as safeguards. More experimental trials are a competing account. F137 Microrobotic Closed-Loop Control Latency At The Limit — Measures the full sensor-to-physical-action delay, in seconds, inside a declared operating envelope. Expert-tuned controls under matched resources establish the comparison. Hidden human intervention and high-confidence out-of-domain action must be ruled out. F138 AI Multi-Agent Sampling Mission Coordination At The Limit — Evaluates calibration-adjusted joint sampling mission completion as a fraction under fairness and safety vetoes. Frozen open-loop coordination and identical observation budgets serve as controls; training-test leakage and missing action provenance are rival explanations. F139 Nano-Quantum Digital Twin External Validation At The Limit — Freezes a digital twin before evaluating out-of-site prediction error against independent measurements. Preregistered experimental design is the reference; additional experiments, leakage or operating outside the authorized action envelope cannot be counted as predictive success. F140 AI Biological Signal Concept Drift Identification At The Limit — Measures detection delay, in seconds, at a fixed false-alarm rate, separating biological-state drift from sensor and calibration changes. Equal-trial expert tuning and hidden manual correction are comparators. Confident action on unsupported states triggers a hard stop. Scope, Falsifiability and Evidence Boundaries All volumes are prospective nonclinical research designs for traceable samples, authorized ex-vivo, simulated or closed-bench settings; no laboratory results, clinical efficacy or quantum advantage are reported.
