## Summary / source snapshot

Non-imaging optical tactile sensors encode contact through changes in optical intensity, wavelength, spectra, or photon counts, offering diverse architectures for robotic touch. Their usefulness depends on whether contact variables can be reliably distinguished from coupled mechanical and environmental effects, and whether the resulting information can be delivered in time to guide action.
