## Summary / source snapshot

Abstract Language-driven grasp detection aims to facilitate open-ended human-robot interaction. However, existing approaches still face three critical challenges: (1) difficulty in interpreting instructions involving complex spatial and logical reasoning, (2) insufficient handling of target unobstructed grasp affordance and collision risks in densely occluded scenes, and (3) limited generalization to novel scenarios due to heavy reliance on annotated data.
