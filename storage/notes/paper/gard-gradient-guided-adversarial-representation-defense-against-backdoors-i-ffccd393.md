## Summary / source snapshot

Multimodal contrastive learning models such as CLIP are vulnerable to backdoor attacks with diverse trigger structures, ranging from localized patches to globally distributed perturbations, with recent attacks increasingly optimizing these triggers in the representation space. Existing post-hoc defenses based on clean fine-tuning may leave backdoors active, while trigger-inversion methods depend on accurately estimating the attack target and trigger.
