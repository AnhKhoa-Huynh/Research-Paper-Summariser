## Summary / source snapshot

Flow-based vision-language-action models can benefit from robot experience collected during deployment, but repeatedly acquiring new physical interactions for policy improvement is costly. Offline reinforcement learning provides a natural way to reuse collected experience, while flow-based policies offer an additional opportunity because fresh generation trajectories can be cheaply resampled from the current policy at replay observations.
