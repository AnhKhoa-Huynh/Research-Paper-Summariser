## Summary / source snapshot

This paper introduces TARMAC, an empirical taxonomy designed to organize physical tasks in laboratory chemistry into a structured action space. The authors evaluate the taxonomy through protocol decomposition and robotic implementations across various configurations.

## Method / approach

A bottom-up analysis of instructional chemistry practice, followed by evaluation through cross-corpus protocol decomposition and robotic instantiation.

## Reported contribution / result

Out of 917 manipulative steps across 23 procedures, 904 mapped to the primitive set, and robotic tests successfully instantiated 8 primitives in five tasks using four robotic configurations.
