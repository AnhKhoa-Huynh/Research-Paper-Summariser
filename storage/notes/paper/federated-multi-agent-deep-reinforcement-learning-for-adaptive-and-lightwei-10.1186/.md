## Summary / source snapshot

Abstract The move toward sixth-generation (6G) wireless networks introduces ultra-reliable low-latency communication and massive edge connectivity, but also enlarges the attack surface and demands security that is adaptive, lightweight, and decentralized. This paper presents a Federated Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning (FMADRL) framework for adaptive secure-protocol selection, in which edge nodes learn local protection policies and share only model parameters.
