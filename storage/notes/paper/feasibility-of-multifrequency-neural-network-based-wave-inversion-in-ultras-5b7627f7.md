## Summary / source snapshot

Ultrasound time-harmonic elastography (THE) is a non-invasive imaging technique that measures soft-tissue stiffness. Relying on the same principle of time-harmonic shear wave excitation as established magnetic resonance elastography (MRE), THE offers full field-of-view stiffness mapping at short acquisition times and sub-second temporal resolution across organs.
