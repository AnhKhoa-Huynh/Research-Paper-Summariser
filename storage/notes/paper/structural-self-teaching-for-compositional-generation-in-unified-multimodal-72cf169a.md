## Summary / source snapshot

We propose Structural Self-Teaching (SST), a novel framework that enhances compositional image generation in diffusion-based unified multimodal models (UMMs) by leveraging their internal understanding path as a source of structural supervision. The generation path of UMMs often struggles with dense compositional prompts, leading to missing objects, attribute binding failures, and spatial errors.
