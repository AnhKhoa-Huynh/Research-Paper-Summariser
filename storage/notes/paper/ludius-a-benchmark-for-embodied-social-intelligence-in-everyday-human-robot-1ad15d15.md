## Summary / source snapshot

Social intelligence in a robot requires interpreting a situation from the perspectives of the people involved and using that understanding to decide when and how to act. We introduce LUDIUS, a benchmark for evaluating how vision-language models exercise this judgment through a robot's verbal responses and actions, organized around 15 social intelligence skills drawn from social psychology, sociology, and cognitive science.
