Collecting diverse real-world manipulation demonstrations is costly, motivating the use of simulation to augment existing demonstrations for robot policy learning. When objects, layouts, or robots change, transferring motions from a source demonstration can produce behavior that is semantically implausible or physically infeasible in the new setting.
