## Summary / source snapshot

Finding valid Control Barrier Functions (CBFs) is difficult for non-linear systems subject to actuation constraints and moving in complex environments. One way to enable analytically derived CBFs is to represent obstacles as supporting halfplanes, allowing the agent to move safely on one side.
