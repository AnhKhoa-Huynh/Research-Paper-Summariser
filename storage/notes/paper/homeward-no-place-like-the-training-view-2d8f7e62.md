## Summary / source snapshot

Vision-language-action (VLA) policies implicitly assume that third-person cameras remain fixed at their data-collection poses; once a camera is bumped, remounted, or swapped at deployment, task success degrades precipitously even though the underlying task and robot remain unchanged. We present Homeward, a training-free, plug-and-play observation adapter that canonicalizes observations from an uncalibrated deployment camera back into the training view for frozen VLA policies.
