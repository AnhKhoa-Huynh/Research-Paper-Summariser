## Summary / source snapshot

Guidance vector fields (GVFs) are a popular framework for tasks such as robotic path-following and multi-robot motion coordination, but existing approaches may be non-differentiable or have large derivatives, limiting their use in controllers that require derivatives of the guidance signal, which is common when the GVF is used as an input in a cascaded controller. This paper proposes an infinitely differentiable constructive GVF.
