# 量子輔助群體重構路徑規劃極限 8/90 QUANTUM-ASSISTED SWARM RECONFIGURATION PATH PLANNING AT THE LIMIT QUANTUM-ASSISTED SWARM RECONFIGURATION PATH PLANNING AT THE LIMIT is a 777-page high-density English research working monograph asking whether quantum, quantum-inspired and classical optimization can plan safe reconfiguration sequences for modular robotic vehicle structures under the same material, computing, energy and time limits. Its central claim is falsifiable. A frozen quantum-assisted pipeline should improve objective gap without worsening holdout Pareto hypervolume or physical feasible-solution yield against strong classical multi-agent planning and optimization baselines. The claim fails if embedding, preprocessing, hardware queue, classical repair, safety violations, selection bias or uncounted execution cost removes the apparent advantage. Thirty distinct mathematical plates cover physical-state graph constraints, collision-free time-space trajectories, support and load margins, actuator work, distributed energy closure, hard constraint encoding, QUBO penalties, quantum benchmarking, objective gap, uncertainty-aware Pareto hypervolume, correlated sensing and physical stop latency. Across 82 chapters and 410 separate study pages, the book develops a testable chain from graph configuration to safe intermediate morphology, inclusive quantum-classical comparison, hardware-in-the-loop execution and independent replication. Two hundred Research Gates specify individual falsifiable questions, blind perturbations, rival models and stopping criteria. Thirty-three Phoenix Answer Embryos propose distinct successor-ready scientific tools and research possibilities. The work is prospective, non-occupancy and hypothesis-generating; no executed quantum advantage, constructed autonomous supercar or passenger safety is claimed. Unverifiable module identity, collision risk, unsupported load paths, invalid physical timing or permission revocation mandate withholding the result and stopping actuation.
