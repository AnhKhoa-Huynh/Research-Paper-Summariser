## Summary / source snapshot

A collision-free detour can still bring a legged robot to the next stair or ledge a few centimetres or degrees off, and a fixed locomotion skill, factory or learned, can stall or fall. Existing methods prevent this with a robot model or extensive trial and error, but on a new site with unknown skills neither is available, so they cannot be used.
