## Summary / source snapshot

CoopDistill is a framework for cooperative driving that combines a potential-game formulation with residual multi-agent reinforcement learning. It uses a curriculum and rollout selector to guide joint maneuvers, improving traffic safety and efficiency.

## Method / approach

A potential-game formulation provides a longitudinal reference combined with a residual multi-agent reinforcement learning policy, a hard-case curriculum, and a comparative rollout selector.

## Reported contribution / result

Relative to the potential-game method, success improves by 2.1 to 27.1 percentage points and delay decreases by 7.3 to 27.3 percent, with no observed collisions across testing scenarios.
