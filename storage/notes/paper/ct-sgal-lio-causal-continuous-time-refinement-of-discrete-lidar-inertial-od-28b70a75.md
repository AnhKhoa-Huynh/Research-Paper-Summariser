## Summary / source snapshot

State-of-the-art LiDAR-inertial odometry methods, including those on richer state manifolds, achieve strong global accuracy, yet their pose stream alternates between propagation and correction and can oscillate over short horizons even when globally accurate. Most existing methods follow these approaches: they either modify the filter to enable faster updates or estimate a continuous-time trajectory from raw LiDAR and inertial data.
