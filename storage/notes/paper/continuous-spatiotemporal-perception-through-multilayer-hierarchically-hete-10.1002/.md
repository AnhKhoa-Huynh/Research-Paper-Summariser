## Summary / source snapshot

The authors developed a multilayer hierarchically heterogeneous fibrous sensor to achieve continuous perception throughout dynamic human-machine interactions. By integrating MXene, barium titanate, and poly(vinylidene fluoride), the sensor coordinates charge trapping, transport, and polarization to eliminate temporal blind spots.

## Method / approach

Fabrication of a multilayer hierarchically heterogeneous fibrous sensor via electrospinning, combining MXene, BaTiO3, and poly(vinylidene fluoride), and coupling it with a one-dimensional convolutional neural network for state recognition.

## Reported contribution / result

The sensor achieved a maximum tele-perception distance of 1.2 meters, stable tactile operation over 40,000 cycles, a pressure sensitivity of 523.6 mV kPa-1, and recognized complete spatiotemporal interaction states with an accuracy of 92.6%.
