## Summary / source snapshot

This paper investigates how cognitive biases manifest when humans interact with multiple AI agents in cascaded decision environments with delayed feedback. The authors designed a naval battle strategy game involving participants and two AI advisors to observe behavioral responses under uncertainty.

## Method / approach

Designed a naval battle strategy game where human participants interact with two AI advisors in a cascaded setting with delayed outcomes.

## Reported contribution / result

Participants exhibited asymmetric responses to outcomes, struggled to identify the true causes of failures, and misattributed responsibility across the AI agents.
