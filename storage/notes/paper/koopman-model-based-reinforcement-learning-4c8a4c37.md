## Summary / source snapshot

We propose a Koopman model-based reinforcement learning (K-MBRL) algorithm for controlling nonlinear robotic systems with unknown dynamics. The K-MBRL learns a control policy by minimizing an approximate policy objective that combines the analytical immediate cost with an estimate of the future cost evaluated at the one-step predicted state.
