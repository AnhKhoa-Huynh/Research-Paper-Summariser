## Summary / source snapshot

The paper introduces RoboARCade, a benchmark designed to evaluate long-horizon manipulation tasks across three robotic embodiments. It assesses agent performance and reports that current leading models still leave substantial room for improvement.

## Method / approach

Evaluating agents on a benchmark of long-horizon manipulation tasks across three embodiments using the MuJoCo simulation backend and interchangeable interfaces.

## Reported contribution / result

GPT-6 Astra achieved 49.8% while Claude Fable 5.1 achieved 43.6%, and agents frequently failed due to poor strategy selection, imprecise execution, or falsely claiming success.
