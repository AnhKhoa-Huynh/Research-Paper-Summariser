## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce SURE, a data-efficient vision-language-action training framework designed to address spatial and temporal uncertainty in robot manipulation tasks. The approach combines a probabilistic action decoder with Soft-DTW alignment supervision to reduce the need for large demonstration datasets.

## Method / approach

Augmenting a vision-language-action backbone with a lightweight transposed-convolutional probabilistic action decoder and Soft-DTW alignment supervision.

## Reported contribution / result

SURE achieves manipulation success rates matching or surpassing baseline models while using only 10% of the demonstration data or 20% of the training steps on LIBERO, RoboTwin 2.0, and real-world tasks.
