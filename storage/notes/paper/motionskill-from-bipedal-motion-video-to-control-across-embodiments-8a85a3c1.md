## Summary / source snapshot

MotionSkill is a framework designed to extract timed walking references from monocular video and adapt them for various bipedal robots in simulation. The system aligns motion data, uses inverse kinematics to map the movement to different robot morphologies, and trains residual policies in MuJoCo to correct joint commands.

## Method / approach

The authors align root motion and body landmarks in a common frame from monocular video, solve inverse kinematics preserving limb directions and target segment lengths under joint limits and closure constraints, and train a separate residual policy for each target biped.

## Reported contribution / result

From initial states held out during training, policies generated by MotionSkill successfully completed 98 percent of trials on the original video and 26 percent of trials when the upper body was occluded for one second.
