## Summary / source snapshot

Introduction A ward-rounding robot that accepts orders at the level of clinical intent becomes a shared clinical resource: several clinicians may hold outstanding requests of differing urgency, and execution is punctuated by confirmation gates at which the robot waits on a human and by engagement steps that cannot safely be interrupted. This paper formalizes the resulting scheduling problem together with the safety layer that it necessitates.
