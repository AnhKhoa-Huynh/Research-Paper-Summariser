## Summary / source snapshot

Recent advances in diffusion policies have demonstrated strong effectiveness when integrated into hierarchical skill-based learning frameworks, leveraging offline data to capture multimodal and temporally abstracted behaviors. However, prior approaches have rarely considered the diffusion process as a learnable component, adopting fixed noise schedules applied uniformly across skills, regardless of their distinct behavioral contexts.
