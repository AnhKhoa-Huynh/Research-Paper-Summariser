## Summary / source snapshot

The proposed system utilizes a Compliant Intervention Interface and a Compliant Residual Policy formulation to improve policy updating and data collection using human corrections.

## Method / approach

The authors introduce Compliant Residual DAgger (CR-DAgger), which features a Compliant Intervention Interface for seamless human delta action corrections without interrupting policy execution, and a Compliant Residual Policy formulation that integrates force feedback and force control.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim that the system enhances performance on precise contact-rich manipulation tasks using minimal correction data, improving base policy success rates by 64% across four challenging tasks and outperforming retraining-from-scratch and finetuning approaches.
