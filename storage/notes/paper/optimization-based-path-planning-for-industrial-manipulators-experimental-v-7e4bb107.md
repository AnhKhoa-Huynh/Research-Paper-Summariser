## Summary / source snapshot

Robotic additive manufacturing requires joint paths that reconcile robot feasibility with process-specific geometry. We develop an offline optimization-based path planner that combines joint-path regularity and self-collision penalties with process costs and constraints, so that a new process enters through its own reference, residuals, and tolerances.
