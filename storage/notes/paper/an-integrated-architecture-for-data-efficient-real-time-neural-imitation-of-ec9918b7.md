## Summary / source snapshot

Nonlinear time-optimal model predictive control (TOMPC) enables highly performant control of constrained systems, but its computational cost prohibits real-time use on fast dynamical systems. Neural network imitation of the TOMPC control law can reduce online computational costs, yet existing work mostly targets quadratic cost formulations in simulation and addresses the challenges of neural controllers in isolation.
