## Summary / source snapshot

This paper introduces a cognitive multi-agent framework utilizing language-grounded hierarchical reinforcement learning for search and rescue operations. It integrates large language models, hierarchical policy decomposition, and a shared cognitive workspace to coordinate heterogeneous agents like UAVs and UGVs.

## Method / approach

A Language-Grounded Multi-Agent Hierarchical Decision Process is introduced to interpret human instructions via a large language model into structured semantic cues for strategy selection and role assignment.

## Reported contribution / result

Experiments in a simulated disaster environment show that LG-HRL improves learning speed by 2.3 times, increases coordination coherence by 37 percent, enhances subgoal accuracy and hazard avoidance by 46 percent and 31 percent, and achieves 20 to 30 percent higher victim rescue rates.
