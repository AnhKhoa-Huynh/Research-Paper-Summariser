## Summary / source snapshot

Optical tactile sensors record tactile videos that capture local deformation and its temporal evolution during contact, supporting robot manipulation, force estimation, and tactile representation learning. Differences in imaging geometry, background appearance, illumination, resolution, and deformation response lead to sensor-dependent observations of the same contact across GelSight, DIGIT, GelSlim, TacTip, and other optical tactile sensors.
