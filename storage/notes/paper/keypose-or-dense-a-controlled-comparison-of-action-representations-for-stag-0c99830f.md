## Summary / source snapshot

Most imitation policies that predict chunks of future actions use the dense action representation, in which the chunk holds one control target per step. In stage-structured manipulation, where a task can be divided into stages with definable endpoints, the keypose action representation instead sets each step of the chunk to the endpoint of the stage that step belongs to, and a speed-capped servo supplies the motion.
