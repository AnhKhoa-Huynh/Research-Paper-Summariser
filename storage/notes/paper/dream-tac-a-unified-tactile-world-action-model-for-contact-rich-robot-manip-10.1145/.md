## Summary / source snapshot

The authors propose Dream-Tac, a unified tactile world action model designed to address the limitations of vision-centric world models in contact-rich robot manipulation. The approach integrates tactile dynamics, visual observations, and actions to improve performance across contact-intensive tasks.

## Method / approach

The method introduces contact-gated visuotactile fusion to selectively integrate tactile signals and a contact-aware attention bias for cross-modal interaction regulation. It also uses a dual-level acceleration strategy with a reformulated contact-aware bias during training and cache-based diffusion acceleration during inference.

## Reported contribution / result

Dream-Tac improves action accuracy by an average of 31.7 percent across six contact-rich manipulation tasks, while achieving up to 2.9 times faster training and 1.8 times faster inference.
