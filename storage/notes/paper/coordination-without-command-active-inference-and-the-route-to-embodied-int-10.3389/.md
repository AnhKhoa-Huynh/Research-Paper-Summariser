## Summary / source snapshot

Embodied intelligence depends on bodies whose contact with the environment, distribution of load and capacity for action are only partly known and are changed by movement. We argue that this creates a practical reason to organize inference through distributed, hierarchical and plural processes, using active inference to relate local belief updating, contextual constraints and action selection within a common framework.
