## Summary / source snapshot

Long-horizon embodied tasks require agents to ground instructions in changing observations, coordinate heterogeneous tools, and recover from failures that can propagate through downstream operations. Although existing LLM-based agents retain experience and refine reusable skills, successful local repairs do not by themselves establish complete-task gains over the current shared procedure library.
