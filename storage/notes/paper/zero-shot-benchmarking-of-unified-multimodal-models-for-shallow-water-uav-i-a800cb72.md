## Summary / source snapshot

Shallow-water UAV imagery provides centimeter-scale observations of benthic environments, but water-induced distortions complicate interpretation. To our knowledge, we present the first systematic zero-shot benchmark of unified multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for both assessing water-induced distortions and restoring shallow-water imagery within the same model framework.
