## Summary / source snapshot

This paper investigates how various modules affect the calibration accuracy of a differentiable neuromusculoskeletal upper limb model. By testing 16 different configurations across multiple movements and subjects, the authors evaluated modules such as muscle synergy and bone parameter optimization to find an optimal calibration pipeline.

## Method / approach

The authors compared different modeling modules—including muscle synergy, learnable bone parameters, optimizable geometry of wrap objects, and constraint torque—within 16 calibration configurations applied to a differentiable upper limb model across 9 testing movements and 4 subjects.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim that torque loss was reduced by 23.6% on average using muscle synergy extrapolation and by 10.9% on average through bone dynamic parameter optimization, leading to the recommendation that both be incorporated into the calibration pipeline.
