## Summary / source snapshot

A robot that works among people must judge what it cannot see: whether an object is still where it was last seen, and whether a pull on a door or drawer is worth its risk. Maps that track object change hold nothing about force, maps of physical properties answer a risk question at a fixed risk level, and vision-language-action models keep no explicit estimate of hidden physics.
