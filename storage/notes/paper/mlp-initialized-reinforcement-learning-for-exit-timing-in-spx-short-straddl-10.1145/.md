## Summary / source snapshot

This paper investigates exit timing strategies for SPX short-straddle portfolios using a combined supervised and reinforcement learning approach. The authors demonstrate that initializing reinforcement learning actors with a supervised multilayer perceptron improves out-of-sample trading performance compared to training from scratch or using traditional exit rules.

## Method / approach

A two-stage framework utilizing historical episodes to generate ex-post optimal hold or exit labels, which train a multilayer perceptron using contemporaneously available state variables, followed by reinforcement-learning actors fine-tuned with a risk-aware trading reward.

## Reported contribution / result

Out-of-sample evaluation over 2024-2026 shows that the MLP-initialized Actor-Critic achieves an average cumulative return of 37.54% and a Sharpe ratio of 1.705, outperforming the supervised MLP alone and Actor-Critic trained from scratch.
