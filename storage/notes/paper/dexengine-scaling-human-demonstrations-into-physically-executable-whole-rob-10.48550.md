## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce DexEngine, a simulation pipeline designed to convert human hand-object manipulation demonstrations into physically executable whole-robot training data. The system positions human motions within reach, computes corresponding arm movements, and applies residual reinforcement learning in physics simulation to correct the trajectories.

## Method / approach

The pipeline positions hand-object motion for arm reachability, generates matching arm motions, and corrects them using residual reinforcement learning within a physics simulation.

## Reported contribution / result

DexEngine processes nine datasets to produce large-scale trajectories and episodes that outperform uncorrected data or alternative correction pipelines when used to train policies.
