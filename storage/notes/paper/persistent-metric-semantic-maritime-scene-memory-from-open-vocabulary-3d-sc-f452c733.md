## Summary / source snapshot

Three-dimensional scene graphs (3DSGs) can provide robots with persistent metric-semantic representations for spatial reasoning, but the existing methods have primarily been developed for indoor and terrestrial environments. In this paper we investigate how a representative outdoor open-vocabulary method transfers to maritime environments and which adaptations are needed.
