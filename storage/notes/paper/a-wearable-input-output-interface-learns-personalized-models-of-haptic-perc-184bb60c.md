## Summary / source snapshot

Wearable haptic interfaces deliver physical stimuli to the skin, yet perceived locations vary widely across users and diverge from actuator locations, particularly for emergent tactile-feedback techniques. We developed a wearable haptic input/output (I/O) device integrating a 36-channel electrotactile array with capacitive touch sensing, enabling stimulation and user-reported percept locations to be registered within the same body-referenced coordinate frame.
