## Summary / source snapshot

Financial documents combine narrative disclosures, accounting metrics, corporate structures, management commentary, and time-dependent facts, making them difficult to represent using conventional chunk-based retrieval-augmented generation systems. This paper presents an auditable, schema-driven methodology for constructing temporal financial knowledge graphs from page-delimited documents parsed using a general purpose document intelligence service.
