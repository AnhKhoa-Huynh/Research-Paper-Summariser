## Summary / source snapshot

We introduce BOP-Refer, the first benchmark for monocular 3D and 2D grounding of everyday and industrial objects sourced from 9 diverse BOP datasets. We pair images from these datasets with VLM-generated and human-verified referring expressions, and derive accurate ground-truth 3D and 2D bounding boxes of the referred objects from their 3D models and 6DoF pose annotations.
