## Summary / source snapshot

A generalist manipulation policy should perform new tasks, operate in unfamiliar scenes, and transfer skills across robot embodiments. We present Wrist Video Policy (WVP), a closed-loop manipulation framework that learns how manipulation unfolds through video prediction and recovers executable motion through geometric estimation.
