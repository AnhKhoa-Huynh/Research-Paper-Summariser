## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce Passivity-Aware Reactive Diffusion Policy (PaRDP) to address the instability and high contact forces caused by high-frequency corrective actions in contact-rich manipulation. The approach integrates three passivity-bounded modules into a slow-fast visual-tactile diffusion base to guarantee closed-loop stability when interacting with passive environments.

## Method / approach

A slow-fast visual-tactile diffusion base augmented with three lightweight modules: a Passivity-aware Interaction Frame & Hybrid Force-Position module, a Passivity-Bounded Fast Force Correction shell using an energy tank, and a Stability-Conditioned Training regularizer.

## Reported contribution / result

PaRDP improves the All score by +0.02 to +0.05 over RDP(Force) and reduces peak contact force by up to 34% with under 7% inference overhead on the RDP/TactAR benchmark.
