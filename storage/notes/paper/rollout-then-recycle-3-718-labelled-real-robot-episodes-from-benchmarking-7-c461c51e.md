## Summary / source snapshot

We release all 3,718 episodes from the ArmnetBench v0.1 evaluation (arXiv:2607.24481), which contains 600 teleoperated demonstrations and 3,118 rollouts of seven imitation-learning and vision-language-action policies on 12 tabletop tasks, recorded on low-cost SO-101 arms; 73% of the rollouts are failures. Every policy for a task was trained or fine-tuned on the same 50 demonstrations and rolled out on the same cell, so the corpus contains many policies' failures on shared tas
