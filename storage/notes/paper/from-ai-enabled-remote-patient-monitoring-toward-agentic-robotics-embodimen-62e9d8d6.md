## Summary / source snapshot

AI-enabled remote patient monitoring is evolving beyond passive data collection toward agentic AI systems that interpret patient states, coordinate care, and initiate action across domestic and clinical settings. This structured scoping review finds that mature agentic care has not yet emerged: Most systems remain human led, while stronger decision-action coupling and embodied AI remain largely confined to prototypes and pilots.
