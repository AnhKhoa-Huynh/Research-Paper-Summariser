## Summary / source snapshot

The paper introduces the Direct Manifold Action Transformer (DMAT) to address latency and execution mismatch issues in visuomotor policies. By modeling action chunks as direct targets from stochastic interpolants, the approach enables single-step inference without iterative denoising.

## Method / approach

Direct Manifold Action Transformer (DMAT) using stochastic interpolants, local 3D geometry, and State-Consistent Spectral-Time Refinement (STR).

## Reported contribution / result

DMAT achieved higher aggregate success rates than Improved 3D Diffusion Policy (IDP3) and DiTFlow3D, while generating action chunks faster and showing robustness under interventions.
