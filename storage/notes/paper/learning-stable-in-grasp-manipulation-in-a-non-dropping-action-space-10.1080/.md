## Summary / source snapshot

This paper presents an approach to dexterous manipulation that decomposes complex skills into simpler components to improve learning efficiency. By integrating constraints and guidance from classical physics and control theory into the learning process, the authors achieve stable and accurate skill acquisition.

## Method / approach

Decomposing dexterous skills into multiple simpler or analyzable components and learning each component using constraints and guidance from classical physics and control theory.

## Reported contribution / result

Incorporating prior knowledge from classical physics and control theory allows for efficient and stable skill learning regarding stable grasps, in-grasp repositioning and reorientation across various objects, sensor and motor noise, latency, and frictional conditions.
