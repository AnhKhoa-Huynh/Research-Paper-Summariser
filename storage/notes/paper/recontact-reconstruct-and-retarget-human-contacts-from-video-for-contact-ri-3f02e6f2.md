## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce ReContact to reconstruct and retarget contact-rich human-object interactions from video for humanoid manipulation. The approach uses an object-centric contact plan and a residual policy to stabilize tracking through occlusions and ensure proper contact forces.

## Method / approach

The method uses an object-centric contact plan specifying contact phases, pairs, anchors, pressing directions, and sticking or sliding modes, combined with contact priors for pose reconstruction and a residual policy over a frozen whole-body tracker.

## Reported contribution / result

In simulation, ReContact achieves the planned two-palm bottom hold in 83.5 of episodes over three training seeds compared to 0.16% for the best adapted baseline, and raises in-workspace tube-transfer success from 20.4% to 97.8%.
