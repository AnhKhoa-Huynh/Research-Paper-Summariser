## Summary / source snapshot

ActDistill is a proposed distillation framework designed to compress heavy Vision-Language-Action models into lightweight counterparts for robotic manipulation. The approach utilizes action priors to guide knowledge transfer, aiming to reduce computational overhead and inference latency.

## Method / approach

A graph-structured encapsulation strategy models the hierarchical evolution of action prediction from a teacher model to a student model, which is further equipped with a dynamic router that adaptively selects computation paths based on action prediction demands under hierarchical graph-informed supervision.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim that ActDistill achieves comparable or superior performance to full-scale Vision-Language-Action models while reducing computation by over 50% and providing up to a 1.67 times speedup.
