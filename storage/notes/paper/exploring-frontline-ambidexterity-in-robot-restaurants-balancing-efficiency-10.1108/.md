## Summary / source snapshot

This study investigates frontline ambidexterity dimensions within robot restaurants by applying the employee-robot-customer service triad and comparing cultural differences between Japan and the USA. Through a mixed-methods observational design, the research identifies four ambidexterity dimensions and highlights distinct cross-cultural variations.

## Method / approach

A mixed-methods qualitative-quantitative observational methodology utilizing a triangulated approach across three stages: identifying frontline ambidexterity dimensions, exploring emergent themes in Japanese and USA service triads, and analyzing cross-cultural quantitative patterns.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim to identify four dimensions of frontline ambidexterity (parallel, facilitation, intervention, and synergy) that balance efficiency and human touch, and they report statistically significant cultural differences between Japan and the USA for parallel and synergy behaviors.
