## Summary / source snapshot

Many embodied world models predict future states in latent spaces optimized for RGB reconstruction, which do not explicitly prioritize object-level semantics and geometry relevant to motion generation. We present MotionRep, a visual representation world model that jointly generates future DINO representations and 3D object flow, coupling semantic and geometric information with object-centric dynamics.
