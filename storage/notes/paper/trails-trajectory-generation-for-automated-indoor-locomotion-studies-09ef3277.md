## Summary / source snapshot

Virtual reality experiments involving navigation and simulated robot motion often require large, realistic environments and numerous distinct trajectories to avoid repetitive navigation while providing sufficient variation for controlled experiments. Designing such scenarios manually is labor-intensive, particularly when trajectories must satisfy predefined geometric and kinematic constraints while remaining diverse and comparable across experimental conditions.
