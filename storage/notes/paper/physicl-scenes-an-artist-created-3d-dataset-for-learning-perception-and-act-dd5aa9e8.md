## Summary / source snapshot

We present Physicl Scenes, a high-quality artist-created and curated dataset of 6,499 3D scenes spanning household rooms, public interiors, and outdoor spaces. The dataset combines detailed geometry, physically based materials, and carefully composed environments with object- and part-level semantic annotations, providing rich, joint supervision for visual and embodied learning.
