## Summary / source snapshot

This paper presents a flexibility-aware manipulation framework that links structural dynamics to sensing, kinematics, and control for robots with flexible links. The authors demonstrate that dominant modal information can define sensor requirements and support real-time robotic manipulation.

## Method / approach

Identify dominant vibration modes using response-convergence and excitation-bandwidth criteria to establish a minimum observable inertial measurement unit configuration, reconstruct continuous link deformation, and apply flexibility-aware forward kinematics alongside a deformation-informed partial differential equation-based controller.

## Reported contribution / result

On a long-reach double-link platform, the proposed kinematics achieves end-effector root mean square errors of 0.0107 m, 0.0194 m, and 0.0102 rad while using fewer sensors than the piecewise-rigid method.
