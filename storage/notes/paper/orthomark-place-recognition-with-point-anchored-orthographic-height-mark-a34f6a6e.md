## Summary / source snapshot

LiDAR place recognition provides the loop closures of Simultaneous Localization and Mapping(SLAM). Perspective from Z-axis, a gravity-aligned scan is an orthographic map of the scene, and a revisit from another position and heading moves this map rigidly in the plane, a motion that a polar Fourier spectrum ignores and phase correlation recovers.
