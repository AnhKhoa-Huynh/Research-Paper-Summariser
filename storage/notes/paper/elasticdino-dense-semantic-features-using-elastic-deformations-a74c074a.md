## Summary / source snapshot

Vision transformers (ViTs) serve as the backbone for countless vision tasks, including dense prediction tasks, such as segmentation and depth estimation, even though the quadratic complexity of self-attention severely limits feature resolution and induces spatial artifacts. In this work, we introduce ElasticDino, a lightweight adapter which produces high-resolution feature maps, adhering to the fine details of the input image.
