## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce VideoAesBench, a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate the video aesthetic quality assessment capabilities of large multimodal models. Through testing 23 open-source and commercial models, the researchers claim that current large multimodal models only possess basic, incomplete, and imprecise video aesthetics perception abilities.

## Method / approach

Evaluating 23 open-source and commercial large multimodal models using VideoAesBench, which consists of 1,804 diverse videos across multiple sources, various question formats including single-choice, multi-choice, True or False, and open-ended questions, and holistic video aesthetics dimensions covering visual form, visual style, and visual affectiveness.

## Reported contribution / result

Current large multimodal models only contain basic video aesthetics perception ability, and their performance remains incomplete and imprecise.
