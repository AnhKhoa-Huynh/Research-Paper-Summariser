## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce FineDex, a framework designed to make finger-level intent expressible and executable in human-object interaction generation and dexterous humanoid manipulation. The approach learns coordinated body, hand, and object motions based on detailed instructions while using a physics-based digital twin to discover physically feasible contact strategies.

## Method / approach

The method learns coordinated human body, hand, and object motions following fine-grained instructions and leverages generated interactions as actionable priors for an agent in a physics-based digital twin, adapting at test time without per-instance retraining.

## Reported contribution / result

Experiments show state-of-the-art human-object interaction generation quality and fine-grained controllability, substantial improvements in dexterous manipulation success, and successful deployment on a real dexterous humanoid robot.
