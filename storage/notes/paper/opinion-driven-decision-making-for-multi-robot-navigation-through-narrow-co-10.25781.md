## Summary / source snapshot

This paper introduces an opinion-driven navigation framework utilizing Nonlinear Opinion Dynamics to achieve deadlock-free multi-robot traversal through narrow corridors without explicit communication. It incorporates a game reduction technique and trained Multi-Layer Perceptron networks to maintain computational efficiency and support real-time human-robot interaction.

## Method / approach

Integration of the Nonlinear Opinion Dynamics model with a multi-robot path planning algorithm, a game reduction technique for decision-making subsets, trained Multi-Layer Perceptron networks to approximate the PRM* path planner, and simulation and game-based human-robot interaction studies.

## Reported contribution / result

Simulation studies with up to four robots demonstrate robust deadlock-free coordination across various configurations, and the human-robot interaction game confirms effective real-time coordination with human agents.
