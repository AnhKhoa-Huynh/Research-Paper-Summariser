## Summary / source snapshot

The authors present KlaskTron, an open-source, desktop-scale robotic testbed designed for physical adversarial multi-agent reinforcement learning. The platform integrates low-cost hardware with a GPU-native digital twin to facilitate reproducible research and sim-to-real transfer.

## Method / approach

Dual CoreXY gantries with high-torque brushless motors, combined with a GPU-native digital twin in NVIDIA Isaac Lab and a learned neural actuator model.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors demonstrate that their sim-to-real baseline enables zero-shot policy transfer, with trained agents exhibiting emergent adversarial behaviors.
