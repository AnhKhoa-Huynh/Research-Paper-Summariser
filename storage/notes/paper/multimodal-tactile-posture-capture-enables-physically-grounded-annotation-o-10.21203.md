## Summary / source snapshot

Abstract Learning dexterous manipulation from human demonstrations requires records of physical interactions that video alone cannot fully capture. Here we present tactile-motion capture (TaMoCap), a portable physical interaction tracking system that records multimodal tactile–posture information during natural human demonstrations, reconstructs physical interaction states of human-object interaction, and converts them into structured semantic annotations.
