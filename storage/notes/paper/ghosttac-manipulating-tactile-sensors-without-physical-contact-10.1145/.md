## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce GhostTac, a contactless attack that manipulates tactile sensors using electromagnetic interference. This technique exploits sensor vulnerabilities to create persistent measurement deviations that can cause harmful robot behaviors.

## Method / approach

Using electromagnetic interference to exploit nonlinear rectification and limited bandwidth amplification effects, reshaping spatial distribution, and manipulating signal magnitude.

## Reported contribution / result

GhostTac successfully manipulated 15 tactile sensors across 10 sensor modules and 2 dexterous hands, demonstrating consistent attack effectiveness and impacting tasks like grasping, slip detection, and material classification.
