## Summary / source snapshot

Atomic disorder offers a potent degree of freedom to tailor material properties, yet the resulting vast configuration space obscures structure-property relations and hinders rational inverse design. Here we introduce Graph Amorphous Diffusion Transformer (GaDiT), a generative model that combines dynamic periodic graphs, masked conditioning and an interval-weighted training-noise distribution.
