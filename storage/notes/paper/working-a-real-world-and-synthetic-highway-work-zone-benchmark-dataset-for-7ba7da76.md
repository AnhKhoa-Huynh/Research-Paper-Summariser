## Summary / source snapshot

Highway work zones are critical edge cases for autonomous vehicles (AVs), combining atypical geometries and jurisdiction-specific regulations that existing benchmarks fail to capture: real-world datasets lack 360° multi-modal, full-scene 3D work zone annotation, and no single recorded site can span the layouts and regulatory variants needed for generalizable models. We present WorkIng, a dual-domain benchmark and training resource.
