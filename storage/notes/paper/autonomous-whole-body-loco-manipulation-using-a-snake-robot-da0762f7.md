## Summary / source snapshot

The authors present a closed-loop loco-manipulation approach for a snake robot, allowing any part of its body to act as both a propulsive mechanism and a manipulation tool without functional distinction. They develop a unified contact-implicit dynamic model and trajectory optimization framework to autonomously push an object to a target location.

## Method / approach

A Moreau-Jean time-stepping framework integrating robot-ground, robot-object, and object-ground interactions, paired with a trajectory optimization over full-order dynamics guided by a geometric planner and nominal proportional controller.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors successfully validate their planner and controller on hardware across various configurations and present simulation results demonstrating object pushing during sidewinding.
