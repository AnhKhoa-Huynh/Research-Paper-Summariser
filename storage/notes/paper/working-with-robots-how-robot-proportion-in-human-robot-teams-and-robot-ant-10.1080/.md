## Summary / source snapshot

Although robots are increasingly reshaping organizational workflows, how the robot proportion in human-robot teams affects employees’ turnover intention remains underexplored. Drawing on conservation of resources theory, we examine whether, why, and when robot proportion exerts divergent effects on turnover intention through two pathways: resource gain and resource loss.
