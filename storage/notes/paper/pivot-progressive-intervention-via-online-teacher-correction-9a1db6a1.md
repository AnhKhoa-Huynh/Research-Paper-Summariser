## Summary / source snapshot

The authors propose a method called PIVOT to address the limitation of sparse outcome-level feedback in reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards on difficult reasoning tasks. The approach combines trajectory-level teacher interventions with token-level supervision to guide student learning more effectively.

## Method / approach

Inserting teacher-generated corrections into student rollouts and applying token-level KL supervision to these segments alongside the reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards objective.

## Reported contribution / result

PIVOT consistently outperforms existing methods across hard logic games and mathematical reasoning benchmarks, nearly doubling the mean avg@8 on logic games compared to the best-performing baseline.
