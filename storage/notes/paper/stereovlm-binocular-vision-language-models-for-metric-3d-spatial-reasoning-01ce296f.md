## Summary / source snapshot

Vision-language models (VLMs) exhibit strong semantic understanding but remain limited in their ability to reason about metric 3D space in the physical world. Existing VLMs typically infer 3D scene structure from one or more images without known camera calibration or cross-view geometric constraints.
