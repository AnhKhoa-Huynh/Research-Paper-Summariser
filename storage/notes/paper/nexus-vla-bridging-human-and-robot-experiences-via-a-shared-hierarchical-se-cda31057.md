## Summary / source snapshot

Nexus-VLA is presented as a hybrid policy learning framework designed to bridge the gap between human and robot manipulation experiences. The approach utilizes end-to-end human-robot joint training and a shared hierarchical semantic latent space to transfer knowledge from egocentric human videos to robot policy learning.

## Method / approach

A hierarchical cross-embodiment semantic alignment paradigm and a shared manipulation latent representation are used for end-to-end human-robot joint training and continuous action generation.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim that Nexus-VLA achieves an 80.5% task success rate on RoboCasa-GR1 and demonstrates advantages in human-data utilization, learning efficiency, and cross-scenario generalization.
