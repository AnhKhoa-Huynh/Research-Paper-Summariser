## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce a style-parameterized predictive control framework for upper-body humanoid robots performing rhythmic movements. This approach integrates kinematic style modulation, beat-synchronous tracking, and joint constraint adherence into a single constrained optimization problem.

## Method / approach

A style-parameterized predictive control framework (SP-RG-MPC) is proposed, utilizing a two-dimensional continuous style parameter to control reference trajectories and shape velocity profile constraints, evaluated via a 10 degree-of-freedom simulation platform in MuJoCo using an unoptimized Python/IPOPT implementation.

## Reported contribution / result

The constraint feasibility rate of the full framework remains above 95% across all tested conditions, and the two style modulation modules play distinct roles.

## Limitations / evidence gaps

The per-step solve time of approximately 197 ms exceeds the 5 ms control period, and the study establishes algorithmic feasibility rather than real-time hardware deployment.

## Why it might matter

The methodology provides an algorithmic foundation for developing safety-aware and behaviorally interpretable motion control schemes for humanoid robots in performance and interactive service scenarios.
