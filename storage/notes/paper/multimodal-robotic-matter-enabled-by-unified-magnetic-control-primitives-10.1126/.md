## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce a unified magnetic control paradigm that utilizes global-field primitives to enable multimodal robotic matter. This framework combines rotating and pulsed uniform fields with field gradients to achieve diverse behaviors in modular robots.

## Method / approach

Using a programmable three-dimensional Helmholtz coil system, the authors applied combinations of rotating and pulsed uniform fields, alongside controlled field gradients, to modular robots with encoded mechanical and magnetic asymmetries.

## Reported contribution / result

The approach enables four locomotion modes, hierarchical assembly of three-dimensional architectures, and rapid shape transitions, demonstrating multifunctional collective behaviors from a minimal set of control primitives.
