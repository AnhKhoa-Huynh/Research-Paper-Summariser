## Summary / source snapshot

Abstract This work presents an event-driven cyber-physical manufacturing workcell that integrates an ABB YuMi collaborative robot with a computer numerical control (CNC) doctor-blade coater for laboratory-scale thin-film processing. Both subsystems retain their local motion-control routines, while Node-RED acts as the supervisory layer and coordinates process progression through high-level commands and reported execution states.
