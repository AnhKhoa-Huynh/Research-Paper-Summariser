## Summary / source snapshot

Robotic Manipulation Surfaces (RMS) reposition objects by deforming the surface on which they rest. A recent class of RMS suspends a continuous fabric sheet from a sparse grid of vertical linear actuators lowering the actuator density of such surfaces but how to design such surfaces so that objects can be controlled everywhere on it, and how to extend it beyond a single module so that objects can pass between modules and achieve parallel, remain open.
