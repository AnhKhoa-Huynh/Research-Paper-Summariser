## Summary / source snapshot

Road anomaly detection systems on autonomous vehicles must function reliably under degraded visual conditions including darkness, rain, and Harmattan dust haze. This paper benchmarks four object detection architectures, YOLOv8n, YOLOv12n, RT-DETR, and Faster R-CNN, under physically grounded lighting and weather degradation, and shows that uniform application of the SCI low-light enhancement network is actively harmful at dusk-level lighting (-16.1% to -30.7% mAP@50).
