## Summary / source snapshot

World models are increasingly used in autonomous driving to predict how a scene evolves under the ego vehicle's actions, supporting counterfactual reasoning and model-based planning. However, existing evaluations focus on prediction fidelity and planning performance, and rarely verify that the predictions depend on the conditioning action.
