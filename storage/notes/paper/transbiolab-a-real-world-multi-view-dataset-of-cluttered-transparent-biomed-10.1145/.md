## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce TransBiolab, a real-world RGB-D dataset designed to address the scarcity of visual data for transparent biomedical objects in cluttered scenes. The resource provides calibrated multi-view sequences with extensive instance annotations to support tasks such as segmentation, depth estimation, and robot manipulation.

## Method / approach

The authors constructed a real-world RGB-D dataset capturing 161,315 frames across 98 scenes, featuring 1.03M instance annotations for 15 laboratory object types, and established benchmarks for segmentation, depth estimation and completion, 6D pose estimation, and system-level robot manipulation.

## Reported contribution / result

The authors claim that existing transparent-object datasets lack the combination of multi-object clutter, occlusion, and calibrated multi-view capture characteristic of real laboratory manipulation scenes, which TransBiolab is designed to address.
