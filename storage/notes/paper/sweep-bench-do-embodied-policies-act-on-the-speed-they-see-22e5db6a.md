## Summary / source snapshot

Robot policies must adapt to the speed of moving objects, yet existing benchmarks rarely measure this ability across diverse tasks and speeds. We introduce SWEEP-Bench, a simulation benchmark with 80 conveyor-belt manipulation tasks spanning 16 skill categories, each evaluated at 13 belt speeds (0.10-0.40 m/s).
