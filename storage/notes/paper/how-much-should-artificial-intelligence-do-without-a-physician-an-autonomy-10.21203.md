## Summary / source snapshot

The authors developed a governance framework for artificial intelligence agents in solid organ transplantation by combining an existing autonomy scale with an agentic overlay. They evaluated the framework against current regulatory and medical guidelines and demonstrated its application using four transplant scenarios.

## Method / approach

The authors created a governance framework by combining Yang and colleagues' six-level autonomy scale with an agentic overlay covering data access, tool use, action scope, and persistence, and mapped it against U.S. device guidance and AMA 2026 agentic AI frameworks using four solid organ transplantation examples.

## Reported contribution / result

The framework categorizes agent functions into four governance tiers based on consequence, reversibility, and autonomy level, concluding that high-consequence transplant decisions require clinician approval and that binding rules must be enforced deterministically outside the model.
