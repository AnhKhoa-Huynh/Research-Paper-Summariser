Scaling robot manipulation learning remains challenging due to the high cost of collecting real-world interaction data. Real-to-Sim-to-Real (Real2Sim2Real) offers a promising route by enabling policy learning in simulation grounded in real-world observations, yet scaling this paradigm across diverse tasks and physical interactions remains difficult.
