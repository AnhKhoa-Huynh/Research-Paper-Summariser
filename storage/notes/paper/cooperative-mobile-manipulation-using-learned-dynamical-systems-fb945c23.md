## Summary / source snapshot

The authors propose CALDS, a framework designed to adapt learned single-agent behaviors for cooperative mobile manipulation. The approach integrates cooperative policy selection and temporal adaptation to maintain task-relevant behaviors while achieving collision-free coordination.

## Method / approach

The framework utilizes Cooperative Policy Selection to identify compatible policy combinations and Temporal Adaptation to ensure collision-free cooperation while preserving the spatial structure and convergence properties of original behaviors.

## Reported contribution / result

Quantitative evaluations across scenarios such as assistive dressing, restaurant cleaning and serving, and drawer opening demonstrate that the framework improves task success and enables safe, coordinated simultaneous execution.
