## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce a thin electronic skin that uses liquid-metal microchannel geometry to achieve high-resolution touch and force sensing with only four resistive channels. A hierarchical mixture-of-experts decoder and a kernel-based simulator are employed to map the geometric encoding into accurate position and force estimates.

## Method / approach

The approach combines an interlocked channel geometry designed via a kernel-based fast simulator with a hierarchical mixture-of-experts decoder to jointly estimate position and force from four resistive channels.

## Reported contribution / result

Across three devices, the sensor achieved a mean localisation root-mean-square error of 0.230 mm, with a representative device reaching a localisation root-mean-square error of 0.228 mm, a force root-mean-square error of 0.043 N, and a super-resolution factor of 58.3.
