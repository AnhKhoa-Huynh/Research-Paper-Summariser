## Summary / source snapshot

This thesis explores how advanced hardware and perception stacks can overcome current bottlenecks in robot learning for contact-rich tasks. The author presents improvements to DenseTact sensors, the MR. CRABS bimanual mobile manipulator platform, and a video-based demonstration synthesis pipeline.

## Method / approach

The author introduces a new DenseTact Mini sensor version, an automated calibration pipeline, and localized vibration and distributed temperature sensing modalities. The MR. CRABS platform is constructed using a TidyBot++ base, depth cameras, a LIDAR, and two robotic arms. The Semantics as a Present (SAP) pipeline uses RGB video to recover object pose, aligns motion across cousin objects using 3D Gaussian splats, and replays trajectories in IsaacLab.

## Reported contribution / result

The author claims that more capable hardware and perception systems enable robot learning to handle complex, contact-rich, bimanual, and semantically rich tasks. Specifically, the MR. CRABS hardware enabled students to complete more complex final projects compared to previous platforms, and the SAP pipeline achieved comparable performance to human teleoperation demonstrations across seven tasks.
