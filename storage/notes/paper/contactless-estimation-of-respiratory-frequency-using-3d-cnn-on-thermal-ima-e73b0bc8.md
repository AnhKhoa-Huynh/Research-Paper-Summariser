## Summary / source snapshot

This study introduces a deep learning approach to estimate respiratory frequency contactlessly from thermal videos. The proposed method utilizes a 3D-CNN trained with data augmentation and transfer learning to bypass the need for extensive pre-processing and manual region-of-interest tracking.

## Method / approach

A 3D Convolutional Neural Network operating on raw thermal video data, trained using data augmentation and transfer learning from synthetic datasets.

## Reported contribution / result

The proposed approach achieves a validation R2 score of approximately 0.61 on both pre-processed and raw thermal videos.
