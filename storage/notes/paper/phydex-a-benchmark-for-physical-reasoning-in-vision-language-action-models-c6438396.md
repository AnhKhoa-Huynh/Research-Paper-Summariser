## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce PhyDEX, a throwing benchmark designed to evaluate physical reasoning in vision-language-action models through dynamic extrinsic dexterity. Their evaluation of three representative models reveals severe performance limitations stemming from pretrained model robustness issues and physical blindness.

## Method / approach

The authors developed a fully automatic pipeline combining grasp generation, motion planning, and closed-loop throw optimization under randomized physical properties to generate 35K trajectories across nine tasks and 100 scenes, and evaluated three representative vision-language-action models.

## Reported contribution / result

All evaluated vision-language-action models achieved success rates below 3%, with failure primarily driven by limited pretrained model robustness and physical blindness.
