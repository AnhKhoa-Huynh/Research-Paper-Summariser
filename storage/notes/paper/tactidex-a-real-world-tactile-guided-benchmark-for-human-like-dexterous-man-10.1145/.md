## Summary / source snapshot

The authors introduce TactiDex, a real-world tactile-guided benchmark designed to transition robotic dexterous manipulation beyond kinematic mimicry into physically grounded interaction. They also propose TactiSkill, a transfer framework utilizing a tri-component tactile reward to translate human demonstrations into physically plausible executions.

## Method / approach

The authors developed a dataset aligning whole-hand tactile signals with multi-granularity kinematic and object states, along with a tactile-driven transfer framework and the TactiSkill framework featuring a tri-component tactile reward for structured supervision.

## Reported contribution / result

Experiments on single and bimanual tasks demonstrate that TactiSkill achieves superior performance in manipulation success and physical realism.
