## Summary / source snapshot

A palpable mass is a common presenting symptom of breast cancer, but neither breast self examination nor clinical breast examination is sensitive enough to detect breast masses early. We hypothesized that deep learning analysis of signals from a single-element (A-mode) ultrasound sensor can differentiate breast regions containing a mass from normal breast tissue.
