## Summary / source snapshot

Service robots are leaving the caged, deterministic world of industrial automation and entering unstructured, human-centred environments. This survey argues that the central transition of the next decade is from machines programmed for fixed tasks to systems that perceive, reason and adapt, and that the pace of this transition is limited less by algorithms than by robustness, safety certification and economics.
