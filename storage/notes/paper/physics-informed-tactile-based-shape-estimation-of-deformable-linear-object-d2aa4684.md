## Summary / source snapshot

Accurate shape estimation of Deformable Linear Objects (DLOs) is a fundamental challenge in robotic manipulation, often hindered by visual occlusions and the high-dimensional configuration space of flexible materials. While Force/Torque (F/T) and vision-centric methods have been proposed, they frequently struggle with grasp-site ambiguities and environmental interference.
