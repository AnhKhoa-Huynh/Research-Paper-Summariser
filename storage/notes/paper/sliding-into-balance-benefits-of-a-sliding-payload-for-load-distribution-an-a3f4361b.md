## Summary / source snapshot

This paper analyzes the cooperative transportation of a payload suspended from two drones in which, instead of being attached to two independent ropes, the payload is allowed to slide along a single rope connecting both UAVs. First, a planar nondimensional dynamical model of the sliding motion with viscous friction is presented.
