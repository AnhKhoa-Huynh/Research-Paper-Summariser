## Summary / source snapshot

We present a four-stage Learning from Demonstration (LfD) pipeline that incrementally constructs a general policy for multi-stage contact-rich manipulation from a single human demonstration. The pipeline is built around a crucial inductive bias—environmental constraints—that separates general task structure from hard-to-model instance-specific details.
