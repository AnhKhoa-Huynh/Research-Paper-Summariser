## Summary / source snapshot

Flow-based vision-language-action (VLA) policies incur substantial inference latency due to their large vision-language backbones and iterative denoising. Asynchronous execution reduces this latency by generating the next action chunk while executing the current one, but the new chunk is conditioned on a stale observation, degrading performance as the delay increases.
