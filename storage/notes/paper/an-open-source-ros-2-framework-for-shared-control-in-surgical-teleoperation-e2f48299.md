## Summary / source snapshot

Surgical teleoperation deprives surgeons of touch, forcing visual inference of tissue forces; the commercial platforms now restoring force feedback remain closed, preventing open research on haptic shared control. This paper provides a system contribution: it presents an open-source, ROS 2-native framework that integrates the compact, three-degree-of-freedom (3-DOF) Haply Inverse3 haptic device with the da~Vinci Research Kit (dVRK).
