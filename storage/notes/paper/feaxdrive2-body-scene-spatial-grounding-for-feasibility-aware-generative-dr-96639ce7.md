## Summary / source snapshot

Generative driving planners may produce trajectories that appear feasible at their reference points but violate environmental constraints over the full vehicle body. We propose FeaXDrive2, an end-to-end generative planning framework that enhances vehicle-embodied spatial feasibility (VESF) through body-scene spatial grounding.
