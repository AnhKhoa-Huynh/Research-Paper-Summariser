## Summary / source snapshot

Visual-inertial-leg (VIL) state estimation offers cost-effective localization for legged robots, adding only a camera to the onboard IMU and joint encoders. However, learning-based leg odometry is limited by its supervision: networks trained in simulation suffer from the sim-to-real gap, while real-world labels require costly sensors or inherit the errors of visual estimates.
