## Summary / source snapshot

The blind perspective-$n$-point (PnP) problem is to estimate the pose of a calibrated camera from 2D and 3D point sets with unknown correspondences. We show that, for complete, noiseless observations, fourteen permutation-invariant image moments up to order four identify a fixed camera pose for almost every 3D point set containing at least twenty points.
