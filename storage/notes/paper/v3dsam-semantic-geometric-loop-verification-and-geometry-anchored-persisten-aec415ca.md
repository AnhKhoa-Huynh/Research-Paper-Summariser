## Summary / source snapshot

To address unreliable cross-view association in long-sequence feed-forward dense SLAM, this letter presents V3DSAM, an open-vocabulary semantic dense SLAM system that establishes physical correspondences at both the scene and object levels. The system combines feed-forward geometric estimates from VGGT-SLAM 2.0 with open-vocabulary semantic observations from SAM3.
