## Summary / source snapshot

The authors propose an online learning approach to model closed-loop dynamics under a frozen locomotion policy for precise loco-manipulation. They address heterogeneous responses caused by unobserved latent conditions using a contaminated Gaussian model combined with a Gaussian process.

## Method / approach

A contaminated Gaussian model with two Gaussian components is combined with a Gaussian process using random Fourier features to estimate response probabilities and weight online updates.

## Reported contribution / result

The approach enables accurate floating-base motion prediction and precise loco-manipulation by capturing predictable responses and reducing bias and fluctuations.
