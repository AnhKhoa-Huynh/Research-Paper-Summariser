## Summary / source snapshot

Human gameplay supplies paired observations, actions, and visual consequences that can supplement robot-policy training. We study event-and-foresight pretraining for InternVLA-A1.5, combining recorded keyboard/mouse event prediction with history-conditioned visual supervision through a frozen video model.
