## Summary / source snapshot

We study robot navigation in indoor environments from fixed exocentric cameras (infrastructure-mounted, external to the robot). We show that standard Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), which succeeds with onboard cameras, fails to learn this navigation end-to-end in our setup.
