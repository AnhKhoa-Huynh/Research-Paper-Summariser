## Summary / source snapshot

This study examines harness design for agentic robots, which combine vision-language models with vision-language-action and world-action models. Through extensive evaluations, the authors investigate how to optimize these system configurations for better task execution.

## Method / approach

The authors evaluate over 470,000 rollouts across eight simulation benchmarks, six vision-language models, and 15 vision-language-action or world-action checkpoints in seven controlled studies.

## Reported contribution / result

Effective harnesses must adapt to both vision-language model and vision-language-action or world-action capabilities, and the reference recipe X-Harness lifts base policies up to 42.8 points across memory, reasoning, and domain shifts without weight updates.
