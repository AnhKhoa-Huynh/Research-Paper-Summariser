## Summary / source snapshot

A small ground robot can carry one camera and a few watts of compute, but the depth foundation models that could turn that camera into a range sensor are vision transformers sized for GPUs. We bring one to a Raspberry Pi 5 with the Hailo-8L, an accelerator that runs every layer in integer arithmetic, attention included.
