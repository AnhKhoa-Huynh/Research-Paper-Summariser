## Summary / source snapshot

3D object reconstruction is essential for robotic perception and manipulation, yet conventional approaches predominantly rely on visual observations that can be limited by occlusion or unavailable in visually constrained environments. In this work, we propose a vision-free, training-free, and category-agnostic framework for reconstructing 3D object meshes from sparse tactile contacts without relying on pretrained shape models.
