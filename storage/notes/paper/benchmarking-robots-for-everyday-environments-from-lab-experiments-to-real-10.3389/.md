## Summary / source snapshot

This study presents an interdisciplinary process for benchmarking service robots in public everyday environments, addressing the gap between laboratory metrics and real-world operation at technology readiness levels 6–7. Three robots were evaluated in three use cases: outdoor park cleaning, pedestrian underpass cleaning, and interactive assistance in a public library.
