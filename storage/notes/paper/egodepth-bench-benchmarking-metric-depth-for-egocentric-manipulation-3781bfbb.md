## Summary / source snapshot

We introduce EgoDepth-Bench, comprising 941,233 synchronized samples collected across 37 distinct physical environments. Each sample combines six calibrated RGB views with metric depth in the native fisheye view, reconstructed from five active-infrared stereo modules.
