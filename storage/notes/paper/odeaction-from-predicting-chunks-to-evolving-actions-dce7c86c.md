## Summary / source snapshot

Vision-language-action (VLA) models typically generate actions in chunks, organizing continuous robot execution into separately predicted segments and thus introducing potential discontinuities at action-chunk transitions. We propose ODEAction, a chunk-free action generation paradigm that replaces action-chunk prediction with Continuous Action Evolution.
