## Summary / source snapshot

This systematic survey examines World-Action Models (WAMs) by analyzing their visual-computing perspectives, spatial representations, and generative dynamics for embodied AI and autonomous driving. The authors establish mathematical distinctions, propose a three-axis taxonomy, and evaluate various architectures alongside benchmarks and physical diagnostics.

## Method / approach

The authors establish a formal mathematical distinction between VLAs and WAMs, introduce a three-axis taxonomy covering Latent Dynamics, Pixel/Video-Generative, and 3D/Occupancy-Centric paradigms, and compare 28 representative architectures, cataloging benchmarks, simulators, and verified leaderboards.

## Reported contribution / result

The paper identifies a divergence between visual generation quality and physical task success, formalizes three physics-grounded diagnostics, and outlines five open challenges with engineering remedies.
