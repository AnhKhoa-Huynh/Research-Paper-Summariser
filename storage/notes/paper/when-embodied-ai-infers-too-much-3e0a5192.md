## Summary / source snapshot

As embodied AI systems enter homes, hospitals, and public spaces, they introduce a new class of privacy risks—not by recording more, but by inferring more. Drawing on modalities such as thermal imaging, radio-frequency sensing, chemical analysis, and pressure mapping, these systems can construct rich behavioural and physiological models of individuals, often without explicit user awareness or consent.
